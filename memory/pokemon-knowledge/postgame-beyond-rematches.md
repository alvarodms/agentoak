# Postgame Beyond Rematches: What Top Hacks Do Differently

**Cycle**: 55 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Findings

After E4 rematches (2 tiers) and gym leader rematches (4 tiers), LoH risks the "rematch treadmill" problem — more tiers of the same content with diminishing returns.

### What top hacks layer on top of rematches:
1. **Battle Frontier** — Emerald already has one; ensuring it works with new mechanics (P/S split, Fairy) is critical
2. **Legendary hunts with puzzles** — narrative-gated encounters, not just "walk into room and fight"
3. **Side quests / missions** — Unbound's mission system is the gold standard; even simple NPC fetch quests add structure
4. **New Game+** — Unbound offers this; technically complex but highly valued
5. **Postgame narrative arcs** — Reborn gives every NPC their own postgame chapter

### Adding new species (Gen 4/5) to vanilla pokeemerald:
- pret wiki has a full tutorial: sprites, constants, base stats, learnsets, Pokédex entries, cries, evolutions
- Each species requires ~10+ files touched (graphics, data, constants, encounters)
- Full evolution lines needed (Gible→Gabite→Garchomp = 3 species each requiring all data)
- Sprites must be sourced/created (indexed mode, 14 colors max, 64x64 pixels)
- **Verdict**: High effort per species. 4 species (Gible line + Riolu line) = 7 new mons = substantial multi-cycle commitment

### Community consensus on postgame engagement:
- Pure rematch escalation is the **weakest** form of postgame
- Players want **variety** — different challenge types, not just "same trainers but higher levels"
- Battle Frontier already provides this variety in Emerald; making sure it works correctly with v2.0 changes is low-effort, high-value
