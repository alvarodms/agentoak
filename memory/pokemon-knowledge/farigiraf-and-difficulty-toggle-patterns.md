## Farigiraf in Emerald Hacks

Farigiraf (Gen 9, Normal/Psychic, evolves from Girafarig) is rare in Emerald decomp hacks because most stop at Gen 7-8. Only a handful include it:

- **Modern Emerald**: One of 3 Gen 9 species added (alongside Annihilape and Dudunsparce). Has improved sprites, followers, icons.
- **Emerald Seaglass**: Includes Farigiraf among its cross-gen additions.
- **R.O.W.E.**: Full Gen 9 dex, so includes it by default.

No hack found that specifically places Farigiraf on Tate & Liza's team — this would be a genuine differentiator for Legends of Hoenn. Most hacks that include Farigiraf treat it as a wild encounter addition, not an E4/gym feature.

## Difficulty Toggle NPC Patterns

**Pokemon Unbound** (gold standard) has 4 difficulty modes: Casual, Difficult, Expert, Insane.
- **Critical rule**: Difficulty can only be made EASIER, never harder. One-way downgrade.
- This prevents exploit patterns where players lower difficulty for hard fights then raise it back.
- If you want a harder mode, you must start a new game.

**Radical Red**: Difficulty set at game start (Normal, Hardcore, Randomizer/Nuzlocke options). No mid-game changes.

**Emerald Imperium**: Easy and Normal modes selectable; appears to be start-only.

**Emerald Extreme**: 6 difficulty modes (Easy through Frontier). Selection at start.

**Community consensus** (PokéCommunity "What's the best way to add difficulty?" thread): Mid-game difficulty reduction is player-friendly and well-received. Mid-game difficulty INCREASE is controversial — seen as either exploitable or punishing. One-way downgrade is the safe pattern.

**Design implication for LoH**: The Petalburg downgrade NPC fits the Unbound pattern perfectly. Placing it at Petalburg (Norman's city, midpoint geographically) means players encounter the option naturally after the difficulty starts ramping. Key: make it IRREVERSIBLE (or at least one-way toward easier) to match community expectations.
