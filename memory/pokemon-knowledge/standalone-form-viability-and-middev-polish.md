# Standalone Regional Form Viability & Mid-Development Priorities

**Cycle**: 216 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: Low-BST Non-Evolving Pokemon Are Collectibles, Not Team Members

No major ROM hack ships a postgame-exclusive, non-evolving Pokemon at BST 300-310 and expects players to use it competitively. The pattern in successful hacks:

- **Radical Red** buffs many low-BST Pokemon with stat increases and new abilities to ensure viability
- **Unbound's Scalemons** mode scales all fully-evolved Pokemon to 600 BST — acknowledging that BST gaps make species unusable
- **Inclement Emerald** and **Modern Emerald** include BST buffs and ability updates for underperforming species
- **Renegade Platinum** explicitly adjusts BST for all Pokemon to maintain viability from start to finish

Community sentiment: players expect catchable Pokemon to be *usable*. A BST 310 postgame-only Pokemon will be caught once, boxed immediately, and viewed as a "dex filler" — undermining the narrative payoff of the Bagon Colony arc.

## Eviolite Is Not Available in Gen 3

Gen 3 has no Eviolite item. Unlike later gens where NFE (not-fully-evolved) Pokemon can compensate with Eviolite's +50% defenses, a non-evolving BST 310 Pokemon in pokeemerald has no mechanical escape hatch. The only viable approaches: buff the BST directly, or give it a genuinely powerful ability.

## BST Sweet Spot for Standalone Forms

Looking at official standalone Pokemon that see any competitive use: Ditto (288, but Transform), Sableye (380 pre-Mega), Corsola-Galar (410 with Eviolite — but evolves in Gen 8). The floor for "usable without gimmick" in a serious ROM hack appears to be ~440-480. Below that, the Pokemon needs a unique ability or niche.

## Mid-Development: Polish > Content Sprawl

Top-rated hacks (Unbound, Radical Red, Inclement Emerald) are praised for *completeness and polish* over content breadth. The hacks that attempt many features but leave rough edges rank lower in community lists. At mid-development (v2.0 mid-arc), tightening what exists beats adding more systems.
