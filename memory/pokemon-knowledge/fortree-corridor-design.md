# Fortree Corridor Design (Routes 119-121)

**Cycle**: 148 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Route Characteristics (Vanilla Emerald)

### Route 119 — Longest route in Hoenn
- Persistent rain weather (3 of 4 days), tall grass, river, waterfall
- Weather Institute (Team Aqua/Magma event) — already has Dr. Hartley NPC (C141)
- Mimic Circle: 6 trainers who copy the player's team concept
- ~14 trainers total, heavy mix of Bug Catchers, Bird Keepers, Fishermen
- Connects to Fortree City; Feebas fishing spot

### Route 120 — Kecleon route
- No special weather, but bridges and tall grass with hidden Kecleon
- Steven encounter (gives Devon Scope)
- ~12 trainers, mix of Parasol Ladies, Bird Keepers, Ninja Boys
- Connects Route 119 area to Route 121

### Route 121 — Safari Zone approach
- Short route connecting Lilycove to Fortree area
- Safari Zone entrance
- ~8 trainers, Hex Maniac, Cooltrainers, Gentleman
- Already has beast sighting NPC (C113)

## Design Themes for Trainer Redesign

**Route 119**: Rain/water ecology. Swift Swim users, Rain Dish, rain-boosted Water moves. Migration species: Totodile line, Poliwag line. The Weather Institute connection makes this the perfect route for weather-aware trainer teams.

**Route 120**: Mystery/camouflage. Ghost, Psychic, and Grass types that hide. Migration species: Misdreavus, Natu. Ninja Boy trainers should lean into deception (Kecleon, Ninjask, Shedinja).

**Route 121**: Transitional power spike. Trainers here should signal "late-game begins." Cooltrainers with 3-mon teams, held items on every Pokemon. Safari Zone proximity means exotic species references.

## What Top Hacks Do With Weather Routes

- **Inclement Emerald**: Route 119 trainers exploit rain — Swift Swim Ludicolo, Rain Dish Tentacruel, Thunder (100% accuracy in rain)
- **Radical Red**: Weather routes have trainers whose teams are built around the route's weather, teaching players to prepare weather counters
- **Design principle**: Route weather should be a gameplay mechanic, not just flavor. If it rains, trainers should use Thunder, Swift Swim, Rain-boosted Surf

## Scripted Event Opportunity

Route 119 + Weather Institute is the strongest candidate for a scripted migration event:
- Dr. Hartley is already established (C141) as a named scientist studying migration/atmospheric correlation
- The Weather Institute is a natural location for detecting anomalous migration-related weather
- A scripted encounter (migration species appearing during rain, or an NPC reacting to unusual readings) would bridge the narrative gap identified in C138's audit
