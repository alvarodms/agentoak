# QoL Modernization Standards in Emerald ROM Hacks

**Cycle**: 155 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Tier 1 — Expected (absence is noticed)
- **Reusable TMs** — Universal across all major hacks (Radical Red, Inclement Emerald, Emerald Plus, Re:Emerald, Emerald Revisited). The single most commonly cited QoL feature.
- **Indoor running** — Standard in Fire Red: Essence, Fakemon FireRed, Gaia, and most modern hacks. Often available from game start.
- **Faster text speed** — Many hacks offer instant text or significantly faster default.

## Tier 2 — Common (players appreciate, not mandatory)
- BW-style repel system (prompt to reuse when expired)
- Forgettable HMs / HM items
- Trade evolutions without trading
- Nature/ability indicators in summary screen
- Increased battle speed options

## Tier 3 — Advanced (differentiators)
- Field HMs usable by wild party members (no teach required)
- Modern breeding mechanics (nature inheritance, Masuda method)
- Mystery Gift events unlocked
- Pokémon following you

## Key Insight
Hacks like Emerald Plus and Emerald Revisited prove that QoL alone can make a hack worth playing. Legends of Hoenn already has strong content — adding Tier 1 QoL removes the "unmodified ROM feel" that undermines the custom content. Reusable TMs + indoor running are the minimum bar; faster text is a high-value add-on.
