# Completeness & Postgame — What Community Values Most

**Cycle**: 274 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: "Complete Package" Is the Gold Standard

Unbound consistently tops every community list (Reddit, PokéCommunity, review sites) as #1 ROM hack. The reason cited isn't any single feature — it's **completeness**: 80+ missions, legendary hunts, Battle Frontier, New Game+, side quests, Zygarde cells. "While others excel in specific areas — Radical Red in difficulty, Infinite Fusion in creativity — Unbound is the complete package." (RetroHandhelds review)

Players value: completion > content volume > modern features > polish. Dialogue polish alone is nearly invisible to players unless it changes a key narrative beat.

## Evil Team Reworks — Untapped Niche

- **Epoch Emerald** (Aug 2025): Made evil team leaders harder but didn't rework their narrative or add postgame resolution.
- **Unbound**: Custom evil team (Shadows) with full story arc but no postgame redemption/reflection.
- **No major Emerald hack** adds postgame villain resolution — ex-Magma/Aqua members reflecting on events. This is an open niche.

## Implication for LoH

The hack's differentiator is migration as living world-building. The institutions (gyms, E4, rival) now reflect it. The next high-impact frontier is making the VILLAINS reflect it — especially postgame. This is both unique and aligns with what the community values (more content, more postgame).
