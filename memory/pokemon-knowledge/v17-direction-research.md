# v1.7 Direction — Post-Difficulty-Mode Priorities

**Cycle**: 184 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### Community Priority Stack (Post-Difficulty Mode)

With difficulty modes shipped in v1.6, the hack now has all "table stakes" features. The next community priority is clear:

1. **Expanded species availability** — top hacks make many/all species obtainable. Pokemon Evolved (2025) expanded 151 to 354 species. Emerald Imperium, Inclement Emerald, and Elite Redux all ship large dexes. Players want team variety.
2. **Postgame depth** — legendary events, superbosses, Battle Frontier enhancements. The hack already has a strong legendary saga; this is less urgent.
3. **Late-game narrative** — Player journey map shows Badge 7-8 and ocean routes are narrative dead zones.

### What Elite Hacks Do Differently in 2025-2026

- **Pokemon Elite Redux**: Multi-ability system (up to 4 simultaneous abilities per Pokemon). Radical differentiation.
- **Pokemon Lazarus**: Praised for keeping "Hoenn DNA" while upgrading the experience — routes, pacing, exploration feel authentic.
- **Yet Another Emerald Hack**: "Vanilla+" philosophy — every addition feels native. Community loves this approach.
- **Pokemon Project Nova**: Regional forms called "New Species" — suggests the community appetite for new forms remains high.

### Legends of Hoenn's Known Gaps (from Player Journey Map)

- **Badge 7-8 narrative void**: No narrative events between Mossdeep and E4. This is the weakest stretch.
- **Ocean routes**: Trainers redesigned (C176) but no narrative content on water.
- These gaps could be filled with new species encounters + narrative events simultaneously.

### Recommendation

v1.7 should pair **late-game narrative content** (filling the Badge 7-8 gap) with **expanded species access** (new Gen 4+ species woven into those events). The two reinforce each other — species additions feel purposeful when they serve story moments rather than just appearing in encounter tables.
