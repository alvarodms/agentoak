# Mid-Game Regional Form Species Selection — Community Evidence

**Cycle**: 208 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Form Density Gap

Legends of Hoenn has 3 regional forms (Corsola_Hoenn, Growlithe_Hoenn, Arcanine_Hoenn) with 1 now mid-game (Mt. Pyre Corsola_Hoenn, C207). Comparable hacks have far more:
- **Emerald Azure** (2025-2026): 116 variant forms distributed throughout the game. Difficulty-focused, actively developed, 6 gyms of story content so far.
- **Blazing Emerald**: ~10+ Hoennian forms starting from Route 103 (first route).
- **Energized Emerald**: 31 regional forms, many accessible pre-postgame.

The trend is clear: form density is a differentiator. Hacks with more forms distributed earlier receive stronger community reception.

## Species Recognition Factor

Alolan Vulpix/Ninetales is consistently cited as one of the most popular official regional forms ever made. The Ice/Fairy type combination was a breakout hit in Sun/Moon. Choosing Vulpix as a regional form base leverages existing player affection and recognition.

Poliwag is less iconic and its evolution line (Poliwrath) already has a Water/Fighting type in later gens, which reduces the novelty of a Water/Fighting Poliwag.

## Route Thematic Fit

Route 113 (ash-covered) is thematically rich for an Ice-type Vulpix — volcanic ash → cooling → ice adaptation is a compelling ecological story that ties into the migration narrative. Route 119 (rainy) fits Water types but is already heavily associated with Feebas as a special encounter.
