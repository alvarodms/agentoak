# v2.6 Direction — Polish Phase vs. Content Expansion

**Cycle**: 282 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## What Top Hacks Do After Major Content Arcs

### The "Polish Inflection" Pattern
Community consensus (across Boundbyflame, ResetEra, PokéCommunity 2025-2026 threads) consistently ranks **completeness and polish** above raw feature count when rating ROM hacks. Unbound is praised as "the most complete ROM hack ever" specifically for its active bug-fixing and polish cycles. Radical Red is praised for being "perfectly balanced" — the difficulty tuning is what makes it stand out, not just having modern mechanics.

**Key pattern**: The hacks that reach top-tier status (Unbound, Radical Red, Blazing Emerald) all had explicit polish phases where they stopped adding features and focused on balance, QoL, and bug fixes. Emerald Imperium's developer explicitly stated their priority as "fix bugs discovered, address immediate feedback for balance, and continue polishing non-bug issues" before adding new content.

### Trainer Team Diversity — "Healthy Spread" Praised
Community discussion (PokéCommunity, FandomSpot) specifically praises hacks with a "healthy spread" of regional forms across trainer teams rather than reusing the same few forms on multiple bosses. Quote: "It would be so easy to only make new forms for the starters and other fan favorites but the spread is a very healthy one." This directly validates Issue #165's concern about form reuse on boss trainers.

### QoL Features That Players Notice
From PokéCommunity QoL thread: reusable TMs, stat editors, ability capsules, infinite rare candies are the most requested. But the deeper insight is that **balance and thoughtful team design** are what make players recommend a hack to others — QoL just removes friction.
