# Bagon Colony Regional Form — Community & Design Research

**Cycle**: 209 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: No Major Hack Has a Bagon/Salamence Regional Form

Searched PokeCommunity, FandomSpot, and general ROM hack databases — no notable hack features a Bagon/Salamence regional variant. Fan discussion exists (one user created "Salamence Twilight" Dragon/Psychic) but no shipped implementations. This is a genuine differentiator if executed well.

## Pseudo-Legendary Regional Forms — Higher Stakes

Pseudo-legendary regional forms are rare even in fan games. The reason: balance. A pseudo-legendary with a new typing and carefully chosen abilities can easily be overpowered or feel gimmicky. Official examples (Hisuian Goodra Steel/Dragon, Galarian fossils) were designed by teams with playtesting resources. ROM hacks that attempt custom pseudo-legendaries without playtesting often get community pushback on balance.

## Community Design Principles for Compelling Forms

From the PokeCommunity "Regional Forms" feedback thread (455914):
- **The type change must tell a story.** Alolan Vulpix (fire→ice) succeeds because the lore is immediately intuitive.
- **Don't just slap a new type on.** The best forms have a reason rooted in the region's ecology or history.
- **Dragon subtype variants** discussed: Dragon/Rock, Dragon/Steel, and Dragon/Ground were all considered "natural" for cave-dwelling dragons. Dragon/Psychic and Dragon/Fairy were seen as more creative but harder to justify.

## Bagon Colony Narrative Hook

Drake's C204 dialogue references the Bagon Colony. Bagon's core identity is "the dragon that dreams of flying" — a regional form that subverts this (a Bagon that adapted to never fly, becoming Dragon/Rock or Dragon/Ground in Meteor Falls) would be thematically resonant and unique.

## Sprite Iteration Timing

Community feedback loops for sprites work best when addressed within 1-2 cycles. @alvarodms' Corsola feedback (sad expression, ghost-purple iris) is specific and actionable — delaying beyond C209 risks the feedback going stale and the contributor disengaging.
