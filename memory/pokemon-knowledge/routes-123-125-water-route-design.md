# Routes 123-125 & Water Route Design Challenges

**Cycle**: 150 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Route Characteristics

- **Route 123**: Land route west of Mt. Pyre, connects to Route 118. Berry garden, ~14 trainers. Mix of Cooltrainers, Psychics, Hex Maniacs. Thematically adjacent to Mt. Pyre (death/spirits).
- **Route 124**: Water route between Lilycove and Mossdeep. Dive spots, Treasure Hunters. ~10 Swimmers. Almost entirely surf encounters.
- **Route 125**: Water route north of Mossdeep, near Shoal Cave. ~8 Swimmers.

## Water Route Design Challenge

Routes 124-125 are **the first water routes** in the trainer quality pass. They pose a unique design problem:

1. **Trainer class monotony**: Almost all trainers are Swimmers, limiting visual variety
2. **Type monotony**: Swimmers default to pure Water teams. Without intervention, every fight feels identical.
3. **Player fatigue**: Emerald's water routes are the #1 community complaint — "too much water" (IGN's infamous 7.8/10 review became a meme for a reason)

## What Good Hacks Do

- **Inclement Emerald / Radical Red**: Give Swimmers mixed teams — not just Water types. A Swimmer might have Lanturn (Water/Electric), Ludicolo (Water/Grass), Sharpedo (Water/Dark) to create varied matchups
- **Type variety within Water**: Tentacruel (Water/Poison, 515 BST), Sharpedo (Water/Dark, 120 Atk glass cannon), Crawdaunt (Water/Dark), Lanturn (Water/Electric) — dual types create meaningfully different fights
- **Strategic held items**: Water trainers with Mystic Water, Swimmers with rain-oriented teams (Castform + Swift Swim users) to differentiate from generic Surf spam
- **Migration species on water routes**: Lapras (migration flagship), Horsea/Kingdra line, Corsola — tie water encounters to the hack's core theme

## Key Design Principle

The goal with water route trainers is to **make every fight feel different** despite the Swimmer class constraint. Dual-typing is the primary lever: a Tentacruel fight plays completely differently from a Sharpedo fight, even though both are Water-type trainers.
