# Steven Rematch Design & the Regi Question (#194)

**Cycle**: 308 | **Date**: October 2026

---

## Official precedents (verified)
- **ORAS champion Steven, first battle** (Lv57-59): Skarmory, Claydol, Aggron, Cradily, Armaldo, Metagross. Fossils are part of it.
- **ORAS champion rematch** (Lv77-79): Skarmory, **Carbink**, Aerodactyl, Claydol, Aggron, Mega Metagross. Cradily/Armaldo are **swapped out** for Carbink/Aerodactyl. Beating it gives Metagrossite.
- **Emerald Meteor Falls Steven** (Lv75-78): Skarmory, Claydol, Aggron, Cradily, Armaldo, Metagross.
- **No official Steven team has ever had a Regi.** Regis are linked to Steven only in the Adventures manga, where he works out how to wake them. They aren't on his canon rosters.

## Patterns
- Game Freak's own rematch changes a few members (2 of 6) and keeps the identity core (Skarmory/Aggron/Metagross). It doesn't swap in a whole new theme.
- Carbink on Steven's rematch is canon. Carbink is already registered in LoH (C305, Roxanne T3+).
- Community view (PokeCommunity threads): putting endgame legendaries on a team ≠ good difficulty. "You don't need endgame movesets and mons to be difficult — good team building is enough." Fights made of legendaries count as tedious.
- Gen 3 Regis are slow, bulky, Rest/Curse walls. Three of them on one trainer brings back the stall-tedium problem from C307 Toxapex research.
- Searches found no named Hoenn hack that puts Regis on Steven's team (searched Oct 2026).

## Actionable
- Rematch tiers: keep Changed Three + Metagross as the core. Add Skarmory/Aggron/Carbink across tiers the way ORAS does.
- If a Regi is used, at most 1 (Registeel fits the "precious metal" theme best), at the top tier only, and with offensive moves rather than Rest/Curse stall.
- A better home for the Regi trio is the Meteor Falls "collector" Steven (TRAINER_STEVEN, Lv77-82), which is already a postgame superboss.

## C319 update (Oct 2026)
- Search again found no hack that hosts a Regi scene at Meteor Falls Steven, so LoH would be doing something new here. Vanilla precedent is Steven's postgame Beldum gift at his Mossdeep house: a story tie-in shown as a gift scene, without a superboss fight.
- Vanilla Emerald already has the Regi unlock (Sealed Chamber Braille, Wailord+Relicanth). A Steven scene should *point to* that existing puzzle (chalk note / dialogue), not replace it or hand out Regis. Then it needs no trainer slot (568/853 stay free).

## C321 update (Oct 2026) — Meteor Falls Steven scene
- **Precedent for a trainer using Regis that the player can also catch:** Frontier Brain Brandon (Platinum/HGSS Battle Pyramid) uses Regirock/Regice/Registeel ("Brandon's legendary giants"). Canon doesn't mind a duplicate, so we don't need extra story to explain it.
- **Manga ownership (PS253-254):** Steven solved the Wailord+Relicanth riddle and partnered with **Registeel**. Regirock went with Drake/Sidney and Regice with Phoebe/Glacia. So the Ice Regi is Glacia-coded, and Steven's role is "the one who read the riddle". Point his dialogue at the Sealed Chamber, not at owning the Regis.
- **Community weight:** The Emerald Kaizo IronMON ruleset makes "E4 + Steven in Meteor Falls" the *win condition*. Challenge players treat this fight as the true final boss, so it has to be the hardest fight in the hack and learnset-legal.
- Search again found no hack giving Meteor Falls Steven Regis. This is still new ground.
