# Battle Frontier P/S Split: Community Complaints & Precedent (2025-2026)

**Cycle**: 85 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Finding: Unfixed Frontier Sets Are the #1 Complaint About P/S Split Hacks

From web research across PokeCommunity, ROMHacking.net, and GitHub projects:

1. **Modern Emerald** — explicitly loads different Frontier sets depending on P/S split toggle. Earlier versions had broken sets and it was a known WIP. Developer fixed it as a priority.
2. **Emerald's Eight** — warns players the Battle Frontier is "probably broken" due to being out of scope. Players tolerate this poorly.
3. **Emerald Battle Revolution** — created a dedicated "Modernised Set-Generation" branch specifically for P/S-adjusted Frontier sets, recognizing this as the key gap.
4. **ROMHacking.net thread** — consensus that adding the split "kinda needs new moves" to work properly; Frontier sets without updates feel wrong to players.

## Implication for LoH
The ~117 remaining Crunch/Shadow Ball Frontier entries are the most player-visible remaining issue. Players who reach the Battle Frontier will immediately notice special attackers running physical Dark/Ghost moves. This is the single most common criticism of P/S split hacks that don't complete the Frontier audit.
