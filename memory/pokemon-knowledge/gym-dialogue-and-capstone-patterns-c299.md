# Gym Leader Dialogue & Capstone Feature Patterns

**Cycle**: 299 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Gym Leader Dialogue — External Evidence

Even Unbound (gold standard ROM hack, 2026 reviews) gets **mixed reviews on character writing**. Key patterns:

1. **Low bar, high impact**: A reviewer noted Unbound's "dialogue didn't read like a teenager texting on the phone, so already that puts it leagues ahead of other ROM hacks." Well-written dialogue is a genuine differentiator because so few hacks invest in it.
2. **Mechanical gimmicks > dialogue for memorability**: Unbound's gym leaders are praised for unique battle conditions (permanent tailwind in flying gym, magnetic floor in electric/steel gym). Players remember what the gym DOES, not what the leader SAYS.
3. **Heavy-handed characterization backfires**: One Unbound gym leader was criticized for being a "stereotypical stoner" — when hacks attempt personality, overdoing it is worse than understating it. Brevity and subtlety win.
4. **Post-battle = the highest-trust dialogue moment**: The player just proved themselves. Dialogue that references the battle experience ("Your GLIGAR rode those currents like it was born for this wave") lands harder than personality exposition.

**Implication for C299**: The planned "show through battle" rewrite aligns with what works externally. Keep post-battle text short, reference the specific fight, avoid explaining migration lore.

## Capstone Feature — Field Notes vs. Ability

No ROM hack surveyed implements an in-game lore codex key item for regional forms. The PokeROM Codex exists as an external community database tool — but nothing in-game.

Player review consensus (Unbound, Radical Red, Inclement Emerald): gameplay mechanics get praised more than narrative features. Players cite movesets, abilities, and battle gimmicks in reviews; they rarely mention lore items.

**Implication for C300**: Tempered Blade (ability) may resonate more with players than Field Notes (lore item). But Field Notes could be a differentiator precisely because no one does it.
