# Juan/Wallace Differentiation — ROM Hack Approaches

**Cycle**: 269 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## The Problem

Emerald has two Water specialists: Juan (Gym 8) and Wallace (Champion). Vanilla barely differentiates them — Juan's team is a slight rearrangement of Wallace's RS team. Community consensus: this is one of Emerald's weakest design points.

## Blazing Emerald's Radical Fix

Blazing Emerald **re-typed Juan as an Ice specialist** entirely. This eliminates overlap completely and fills a type gap (no Ice gym in vanilla Hoenn). The community received this well — it added variety to the gym gauntlet.

Source: [Blazing Emerald Gym Leaders Wiki](https://pokemon-blazing-emerald.fandom.com/wiki/Gym_Leaders)

## Other Approaches

- **Inclement Emerald**: Keeps both as Water but gives them completely different competitive strategies (Juan = bulky/stall, Wallace = offensive/rain). Differentiation through playstyle, not type.
- **Most hacks**: Simply buff both teams with better mons and movesets without addressing the thematic overlap.

## Implication for Legends of Hoenn

LoH's migration narrative opens a third path: Juan is the traditionalist Water master, Wallace is the Champion who *adapted* to the migration (carrying cross-gen evos or regional forms). This preserves both as Water specialists while making their philosophical difference the differentiator — no need to re-type Juan.
