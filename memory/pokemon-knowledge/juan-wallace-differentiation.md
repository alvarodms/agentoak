# Juan/Wallace Differentiation — ROM Hack Approaches

**Cycle**: 272 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## The Problem

Emerald has two Water specialists: Juan (Gym 8) and Wallace (Champion). Vanilla barely differentiates them — Juan's team is a slight rearrangement of Wallace's RS team. Community consensus: this is one of Emerald's weakest design points.

## The "LOL Thunderbolt" Problem (C272 research)

Community complaints go beyond thematic overlap — the mechanical repetition is the deeper issue. As one widely-cited complaint puts it: "4/6 of Wallace's team succumbs to 'LOL Thunderbolt'" while Steven's diverse team (Metagross, Armaldo, Cradily) demanded varied counterplay. Players who beat Juan with an Electric-type use the exact same strategy against Wallace hours later. The Atrocious Gameplay Wiki lists Wallace-as-Champion specifically because of this.

Source: [Atrocious Gameplay Wiki — Wallace](https://atrociousgameplay.miraheze.org/wiki/Wallace_(Pokemon_Emerald)), [Serebii Forums](https://forums.serebii.net/threads/why-does-the-champion-change-in-emerald.496518/), [GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/792673-pokemon-omega-ruby/70523440)

## Blazing Emerald's Radical Fix

Blazing Emerald **re-typed Juan as an Ice specialist** entirely. Eliminates overlap and fills a type gap (no Ice gym in vanilla Hoenn). Community received well.

## Other Approaches

- **Inclement Emerald**: Keeps both as Water but gives completely different competitive strategies (Juan = bulky/stall, Wallace = offensive/rain). Differentiation through playstyle, not type.
- **Most hacks**: Simply buff both teams without addressing overlap.

## Key Design Insight for LoH

Differentiation must be **mechanical, not just narrative**. If both leaders still fold to Thunderbolt, the "traditionalist vs. adapter" story doesn't save the gameplay experience. Wallace's migration-adapted team needs mons that *resist* or are *neutral* to Electric — regional forms, cross-gen evos, or dual types that break the "one move sweeps all" pattern. Juan can remain the pure Water traditionalist precisely because Wallace will force a different strategy.

## C272 Implementation — SHIPPED

**Juan**: 5-mon teams across all 5 tiers. Corsola_Hoenn (Ghost/Rock) + Starmie, Whiscash, Crawdaunt, Kingdra ace. 2/5 Electric-weak. Dialogue: quiet fisherman who noticed the coral changing.

**Wallace**: 6-mon teams across all 5 tiers. 3 migration species (Ludicolo_Hoenn Electric/Grass, Arcanine_Hoenn Water/Fire, Ninetales_Hoenn Ice/Fairy) + Whiscash→Swampert (T3+), Gyarados, Milotic ace. 3/6 Electric-weak but Mirror Coat punishes Thunderbolt, Ludicolo_Hoenn quad-resists it, Whiscash/Swampert are immune. Dialogue: aesthetic collector who curated a gallery of Hoenn's new forms.

**Zero shared species** except Whiscash. Zero shared aces. Zero shared migration forms.
