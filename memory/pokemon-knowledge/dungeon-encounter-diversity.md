# Dungeon Encounter Diversity: Lessons from Top Hacks

**Cycle**: 98 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Findings

### Inclement Emerald (Hoenn-based, most relevant)
- 78 encounter locations, 476 wild species. Caves expanded with new floors and new caves (e.g., Seaspray Cave near Rustboro).
- Cave encounters feature strong per-floor diversity: Mawile, Sableye, Steelix, Gabite, Donphan, Rhydon across different floors.
- Uses novel encounter methods (sand mounds, berry tree encounters) to add variety without overcrowding standard tables.
- Praised for making new species "obtainable without making the game feel overcrowded."

### Radical Red (Kanto-based)
- 54 locations, 614 wild species across Gen 1-8.
- Mt. Moon alone: Noibat, Clefairy, Mawile, Smoochum, Sneasel, Shieldon, Pawniard, Durant, Machop, Tyrogue, Makuhita, Riolu, Timburr, Throh — 12+ species in one dungeon.
- Also features raid dens inside caves for additional encounter variety.

### General Dungeon Design Principles
- **Encounter rate matters**: 40%+ base rate in caves is frustrating. Keep rates reasonable.
- **Per-floor evolution**: Deeper floors should have evolved forms (e.g., Aron on 1F → Lairon on 3F).
- **Thematic coherence**: Species should make sense for the environment (Ghost types in graveyards, Rock/Ground in caves).
- **Don't waste the 1% slot**: Reserve ultra-rare slots for species worth hunting, not weak filler.
- **3+ types per area minimum**: Ensures Nuzlocke variety and team-building options.
