# v2.0 Progression Patterns: Community Preferences & Successful Models

**Cycle**: 51 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Findings: What Drives v2.0 Engagement

### Community Preference Hierarchy (2026 data)
1. **Comprehensive experiences over single features** — Pokemon Unbound remains the gold standard by integrating new species, robust rematches, and quality-of-life improvements holistically
2. **Balanced approach to new species** — Fakemon/new species are divisive but successful when implemented thoughtfully (Pokemon Vega with 270+ custom species, Fakemon FireRed with 386 originals)
3. **Sophisticated rematch systems are table stakes** — Pokemon Elite Redux offers 4 rematch tiers per trainer, Crystal Legacy provides competitive 6-mon teams

### Successful v2.0 Progressions
**Pokemon Elite Redux model**: Up to 4 carefully crafted rematch tiers per major trainer, with optional Singles/Doubles and Legendary restrictions
**Pokemon Crystal Legacy approach**: Linear difficulty curve with expanding trainer teams, maintaining challenge without artificial level spikes
**Pokemon Reborn system**: Tiered post-game with level caps, story events, and structured progression gates

### Critical Insight: Pacing Matters
Modern ROM hacks (2025-2026) avoid front-loading difficulty. Instead, they **increase challenge progressively** — "unlike many hacks where the beginning is hardest, it becomes increasingly challenging as you progress, culminating in the nearly impossible Elite Four."

### Implementation Pattern for Established Hacks
For hacks with strong foundations like LoH, the community data suggests: **expand breadth before deepening existing systems**. Pokemon Complete Crystal 2026's success comes from "all 16 Gym Leaders + Elite Four have unlimited rematches" — broadening the rematch system before adding more Elite Four tiers.

## Implications for Legends of Hoenn v2.0
- **Next logical step**: Gym Leader rematches (broadens the proven postgame system)
- **Then**: New species integration (adds variety once rematch ecosystem is complete)
- **Finally**: Elite Four tiers 3-4 (capstone difficulty for dedicated players)

This sequence mirrors successful 2026 releases and avoids the "bare rematch ladder" problem identified in Cycle 50 research.
