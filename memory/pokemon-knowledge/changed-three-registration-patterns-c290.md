**Cycle**: 290 | **Date**: April 2026

## Pure Steel vs Grass/Steel — Evidence from the Scene

The C289 research flagged a discrepancy: the spec says Treecko_Hoenn is **Pure Steel** ("Crystal Gecko") but strategy-notes says "Grass/Steel?" with question marks.

Community evidence strongly favors **Pure Steel** for this design:

1. **Moveset alignment**: The spec's entire learnset is Steel moves (Metal Claw, Steel Wing, Iron Head, Meteor Mash) with zero Grass moves. Adding Grass typing would create expectation of Grass STAB that the learnset doesn't deliver.
2. **Radical Red precedent**: Radical Red changed Mega Sceptile from SpA-focused to Atk-focused with Technician ability. The takeaway: when you re-type a species, the *entire identity* must shift. Half-measures (keeping Grass typing but removing all Grass moves) confuse players.
3. **Ephemerald's 382-form approach**: Every type combo represented, and community noted forms felt "out of place" when typing didn't match movepool/ability. Coherence is king.
4. **Competitive niche**: Pure Steel at 120 Speed with Clear Body fills a role no Gen 3 mon occupies — fast Steel physical sweeper with 11 resistances. Grass/Steel at 120 Spe would overlap more with existing niches and add a crippling 4x Fire weakness that undermines the "crystal armor" fantasy.

**Recommendation**: Lock in Pure Steel. The design is internally coherent as-is.

## Ephemerald — Full Regional Forme Hack Lessons

Pokemon Ephemerald (382 Sapphosian Formes) is the closest community parallel to Legends of Hoenn's ambition. Key lessons:
- Every form has new types + movepools + abilities (not just reskins)
- Community praised coherent designs, criticized forms that felt arbitrary
- Modified the type chart itself (Ice gained resistances, Poison hits Dragon SE)

## Mass Registration Risk

9 species × 27 files is the largest single registration batch attempted. No evidence of other decomp projects doing this in one pass — most add species incrementally. The `make check_all_quick` tool (deferred 3 cycles) would pay off here.
