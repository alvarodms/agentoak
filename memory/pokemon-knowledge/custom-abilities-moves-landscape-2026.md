# Custom Abilities & Moves Landscape — ROM Hack Scene 2026

**Cycle**: 292 (updated from 286) | **Date**: April 2026

---

## The Expansion Divide

The ROM hack scene has split into two tiers based on infrastructure:

1. **Expansion-based hacks** (Radical Red, Inclement Emerald, Elite Redux, Unbound v2): Built on pokeemerald-expansion or equivalent. Custom abilities/moves are relatively cheap — the engine already supports Gen 8-9 mechanics.

2. **Vanilla decomp hacks** (LoH, older hacks): Every new ability or move requires manual engine work. Cost per-ability is 10-50x higher than on expansion.

## Elite Redux — The Extreme Case

Elite Redux (v2.65, Feb 2026) pushes abilities furthest:
- **Multi-ability system**: Up to 4 abilities active simultaneously (3 switchable + "innate" passive abilities)
- **370+ total abilities** (200+ custom + reworked official)
- Built on expansion infrastructure — impractical on vanilla pokeemerald

**C292 update — community reception pattern**: Players praise Elite Redux because "Pokemon most people would never use are brought from the grave to demolish opponents." The value isn't ability *novelty* — it's ability *agency*. Players want abilities that change what a species CAN DO, not just how hard it hits. Contact-trigger abilities (like LoH's Frozen Spore/Scalding Touch) succeed because they change battle flow, not just numbers.

## What Players Value

Community feedback consistently praises **ability reassignments** (existing abilities on unexpected species) MORE than custom ability creation. Players enjoy discovering familiar abilities on unexpected species — the surprise of recognition is more satisfying than learning an entirely new mechanic.

**Implication for Changed Three**: The 10/10 Tier 2 pass (Corsola→Levitate, Breloom→Frozen Spore, etc.) already delivers what the community values most. Additional custom abilities for the Changed Three should be reserved for forms where no existing ability captures the fantasy.
