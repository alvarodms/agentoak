# Villain Remnant Trainer Design Patterns

**Cycle**: 122 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Findings

### What Makes Grunt Battles Not-Boring

The #1 complaint about villain teams in mainline games AND ROM hacks is repetitive grunt rosters (Zubat/Poochyena spam). The best hacks solve this by:

1. **Diverse team comps per grunt** — Each grunt should feel like a person with a specialty, not a copy-paste. Radical Red gives even minor trainers custom movesets with competitive coverage.
2. **Theme over mono-type** — Magma grunts don't all need pure Fire/Ground. A "demolitions expert" might run Electrode + Camerupt. An "excavation grunt" might have Sandslash + Mightyena. Personality through team comp.
3. **Competitive movesets at appropriate scale** — Postgame grunts in Radical Red/Inclement Emerald have held items and EV-trained mons. For Legends of Hoenn's difficulty tier, at minimum give held items and 2-move coverage.
4. **Narrative purpose** — The best grunt encounters explain WHY they're there. "Remnants" implies these are holdouts who didn't disband — fanatics, loyalists, or scavengers. Their dialogue should reflect motivation.

### Postgame Villain Encounter Patterns

- **Unbound**: Post-game villain encounters are tiered by area difficulty, not uniform
- **Radical Red**: Even minor trainers have 3-team random selection near endgame
- **Inclement Emerald**: Custom AI and movesets for all trainers, grunts included

### Composition Advice for Magma/Aqua Remnants (Lv40-45)

**Magma remnants** (Terra Cave): Should use Ground/Fire plus dark-type muscle (Mightyena). Consider Camerupt (signature), Torkoal (defense), Mightyena (intimidate), Golbat.

**Aqua remnants** (Seafloor Cavern): Should use Water plus Mightyena/Golbat staples. Consider Sharpedo (signature aggressive), Crawdaunt, Mightyena, Golbat/Crobat.

Give each grunt 3-4 Pokémon with held items. Admin-tier trainers get 4-5 with better items and coverage moves.
