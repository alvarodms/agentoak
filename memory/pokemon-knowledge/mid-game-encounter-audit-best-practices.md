# Mid-Game Encounter Audit: Best Practices from Top Hacks

**Cycle**: 91 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Principles for Routes 110-113 Audit

### 1. The "Team-Building Sweet Spot"
Mid-game routes (badges 3-5) are where players finalize their core team. Top hacks (Gaia, Inclement Emerald, Drayano's series) ensure every route offers at least one species worth catching — not filler.

### 2. Avoid Over-Stuffing
Radical Red criticism: "Good luck getting everything... the game fails to account for how small Kanto is, and expects you to get 10 new mons every route." For a vanilla-sized Emerald hack, 8-10 species per route is the sweet spot. More than 12 feels bloated; fewer than 6 feels barren.

### 3. Gym-Prep Encounters
Best hacks place species that counter upcoming gym leaders on preceding routes. Routes 110-113 lead into Flannery (Fire) and Norman (Normal). Water/Ground types on these routes give players tools without being heavy-handed.

### 4. Nuzlocke-Friendly Design
- No "dead" encounters — every species should be usable at that game stage
- Don't put the weakest species at 1% rarity (wastes the encounter)
- Ensure at least 3 different types per route for Nuzlocke variety
- Pokémon Gaia praised specifically for giving "brand-new set of interesting critters" every route

### 5. Migration Species Integration
LoH's unique angle: foreign species appearing alongside natives. Best approach: migrants should fill type gaps that vanilla Hoenn lacks on those routes, not duplicate existing coverage. Each migrant should feel like a discovery, not clutter.

### 6. Consistent Difficulty Curve
Both Radical Red and Inclement Emerald praised for "same stream of difficulty throughout the entire game." Mid-game encounter levels should match the target curve (Lv16-22 for Routes 110-113) without sudden spikes.

### 7. Evolved Forms Appearing
Mid-game is when players should start seeing occasional evolved forms in the wild (stage 1 evolutions). This signals progression and gives catch-up options for players who lost team members.
