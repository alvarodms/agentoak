# Postgame Rival Battle — Community Sentiment & Design Patterns

**Cycle**: 277 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## The Emerald Rival Gap

Brendan/May's last battle in vanilla Emerald is on Route 128 (before Victory Road). Community consistently identifies this as a major weakness: "very disappointing rivals that quit halfway through and didn't even bother evolving their starters to final forms." The rival simply vanishes from the player's journey right when the story climaxes.

**ORAS recognized this gap**: Game Freak added a new "final" May/Brendan battle right after the Pokémon League in the remakes, confirming the design need. However, in ORAS you still cannot rebattle them postgame — the last encounter is one-and-done.

## How ROM Hacks Handle It

- **Inclement Emerald**: Postgame May has Mega Blaziken + full competitive team. Mechanically escalated, no narrative justification.
- **Unbound**: Custom rival (not Brendan/May) with a full story arc but postgame battles are mission-gated, not location-tied.
- **Emerald Squared**: Postgame rematches with key trainers, though details on rival specifics are sparse.
- **Elite Redux**: Multi-ability difficulty system with postgame boss gauntlets including rival.
- **General pattern**: Most hacks add postgame rival rematches as harder versions of the same team. Very few tie the rematch to a narrative beat or location with emotional resonance.

## Design Considerations

- Level 55 (as suggested in issue #161) is LOW for postgame — most hacks place postgame trainers at 70-80+. Lv.55 would feel like a mid-game fight.
- Littleroot Town is an emotionally resonant location — "where it all began" closure.
- The gap between Lilycove and Champion is bigger in LoH than vanilla because the rival arc was meaningfully redesigned in v2.4.
- A postgame rival fight is an opportunity to showcase Changed Three species on the rival's team — proving the migration affected them too.
