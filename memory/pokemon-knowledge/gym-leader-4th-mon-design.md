# Gym Leader 4th Mon Design — Implemented C173

## Design Pattern
The 4th mon fills a role gap in each team. Inserted as 2nd slot to create a "mid-boss" buffer.

## Implemented Additions

| Leader | 4th Mon | Role | Key Threat |
|--------|---------|------|------------|
| Flannery | Torkoal Lv26 (White Herb) | Bulky wall, Amnesia+Yawn stall | Blocks physical sweepers; Yawn forces switches |
| Norman | Zangoose Lv29 (Silk Scarf) | Physical setup sweeper | Swords Dance+Slash is lethal if unchecked; Shadow Claw covers Ghosts |
| Winona | Tropius Lv30 (Sitrus Berry) | Grass/Flying mixed | Solar Beam+Sunny Day combo; Earthquake threatens Electric counters |
| Juan | Whiscash Lv43 (Lum Berry) | Ground/Water tank | Earthquake immune to Electric; Rest+Lum Berry sustain |

## Level Adjustments

- Winona's Altaria: 31→32 (maintain spacing with new Tropius at 30)

## Rematch Consistency
All 4 leaders' rematch T1 parties (Lv38-41, 4 mons) remain stronger than the expanded base teams. No regression.
