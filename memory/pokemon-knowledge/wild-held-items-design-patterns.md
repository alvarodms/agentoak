# Wild Held Items: Design Patterns from Top Hacks

**Cycle**: 93 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Findings

### Compound Eyes Interaction (Critical for Gen 3)
- Compound Eyes in the lead slot boosts wild held item rates from 50%/5% to 60%/20%
- In Gen 3, Nincada (Route 116) is the accessible CompoundEyes user; Butterfree also has it and learns Thief
- This creates a player-driven farming loop: discovery → farming setup → reward
- LoH already has Nincada available — players CAN exploit this if items are worth farming

### What Top Hacks Do
- **Radical Red**: Frisk boosts held item chances alongside CompoundEyes; DexNav layers discovery incentives on wild encounters; consumable items return after trainer battles but NOT wild battles (preserves wild item value)
- **Inclement Emerald**: Marts sell common competitive items (Focus Sash, Gems) so wild-found items maintain unique value for rarer finds
- **Refined Gold**: "Overwhelming amount" of wild held items — essentially Vanilla+ philosophy of making every catch feel rewarding

### Design Principles
1. **Rare items should feel like discoveries** — Moon Stone on Clefairy, Amulet Coin on Meowth before it's purchasable
2. **Common items should be useful at that game stage** — Oran/Rawst Berries early, type-boost items mid-game
3. **Thief creates agency** — players who notice held items can steal them without catching, adding tactical depth
4. **CompoundEyes farming is an opt-in reward loop** — knowledgeable players get more value from the system
