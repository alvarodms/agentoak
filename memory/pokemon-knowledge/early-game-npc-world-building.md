# Early-Game NPC World-Building in ROM Hacks

**Cycle**: 140 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### What Top Hacks Do Differently in the First Hours

**Unbound** — Players describe it as "playing an entirely new Game Freak game." The key: every NPC has a purpose tied to the region's lore (war-torn Borrius). Even early-town NPCs reference the setting's history, creating immediate sense of place. Difficulty levels let casual players enjoy the world-building without frustration.

**Inclement Emerald / Elite Redux** — Built on Emerald's semi-open structure. The developer explicitly noted "Hoenn is not Kanto and is considerably less linear," meaning early-game NPCs need to work regardless of which order players reach areas. Flavor text must be location-specific, not sequence-dependent.

**Radical Red** — Strong QoL (auto-run, nature changer, portable healing) but criticized for Kanto feeling cramped with too many Pokemon crammed in. Lesson: encounter variety is good, but world-building context for WHY unusual species appear matters more than raw availability.

### NPC Dialogue Best Practices (PokeCommunity consensus)

1. **Consistency** — Same formatting, capitalization, and tone across all NPCs
2. **Occupation-specific voice** — A fisherman, a scientist, and a child should describe the same phenomenon differently
3. **Character bios** — Even minor NPCs benefit from answering "who is this person, what do they care about"
4. **Short and punchy** — 2-3 text boxes max for flavor NPCs. Players stop reading walls of text.
5. **Reference concrete details** — Name actual species, actual locations. "I saw a Riolu near Route 116" beats "I saw something strange"

### Critical Anti-Pattern: Generic "Something Strange" Dialogue

The worst early-game NPCs say vague things like "Something weird is happening." The best ones filter observations through their occupation and location:
- Bug Catcher: "The Caterpie on Route 102 are acting skittish — like something unfamiliar moved in"
- Devon Scientist: "Our sensors detected non-native species signatures near Rustboro"
- Flower shop owner: "New pollinators I've never seen before — my Berries are thriving!"

Each NPC sees the same migration through their own lens. This is the Unbound/Gaia pattern that reviewers consistently praise.
