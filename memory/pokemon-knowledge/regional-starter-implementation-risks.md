# Regional Starter Implementation — Community Patterns & Risks

**Cycle**: 275 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Radical Red's Seviian Form Quarantine (Key Finding)

Radical Red deliberately quarantines all custom regional forms (Seviian Forms) from the main game. Only one is available pre-postgame (an egg after Erika). Trainers in the main game never use them. Rationale: avoid jarring players who dislike custom Pokémon.

**LoH contrast**: LoH has already committed to the opposite philosophy — migration forms appear on gym leaders (C268-272), villain teams (C248-251), E4/Champion (C205-208), and the rival (C273). The quarantine ship has sailed. Regional starters as rare wild encounters (4%) are consistent with this integrated identity.

## 4% Encounter Rate — "Hunt for It" Territory

From Cycle 255 research: 4% in a 12-slot encounter table means ~25 encounters to find one. Most casual players won't encounter them organically unless they're specifically exploring those areas.

**The postgame Birch gift is the critical safety net.** Without it, a majority of players would never see these species. The wild encounter is the "discovery moment" for explorers; the gift is the access guarantee.

## Blazing Emerald Starter Approach

Blazing Emerald doesn't use regional Hoenn starters — it swaps the starter trio entirely (Clefairy/Eevee/Pikachu with 28 alternatives). Its "Hoennian Forms" are separate. This means LoH's approach of keeping its signature pseudo-legendary starters while adding regional Hoenn starters as wild species is genuinely novel — no major hack does exactly this.

## Community Anti-Pattern: Unpolished Custom Species

PokéCommunity "bloated" criticism (see early-game-form-thesis-statement.md) applies to rushed regional forms. The top complaints: bad sprites, unbalanced stats, moves that don't exist in the engine. LoH has already confirmed all moves exist in the hack's table — the main risk is sprite quality for 9 species.
