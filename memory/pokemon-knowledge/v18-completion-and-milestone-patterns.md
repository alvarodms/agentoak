# v1.8 Completion & Milestone Release Patterns

**Cycle**: 200 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### Incremental Postgame Shipping — Emerald Squared Model
Pokemon Emerald Squared shipped its main game first, then added postgame content (Agartha region, legendary encounters, trainer rematches) via a "Patch 2.0" update. This iterative approach was well-received — players accepted that postgame was delivered incrementally. The community didn't punish the hack for shipping without postgame; they praised the update when it arrived.

**Implication**: Shipping v1.8 with 2/4 quests and adding the remaining 2 in v1.9 is a valid approach. Each release should be internally consistent — don't promise 4 quests and deliver 2.

### Community Preference: Complete > Ambitious
Across 2025-2026 "best ROM hack" lists, the consistently praised hacks (Unbound, Radical Red, Crystal Clear, Gaia) are all **complete**. Partially completed ambitious hacks (like the discontinued Torzach) are cautionary tales. Players tolerate smaller scope far better than incomplete scope.

**Implication**: Better to declare v1.8 "done" with 2 quests + 2 regional forms as a self-contained update, then plan Quests 3-4 as v1.9 features.

### What Makes Postgame Engaging (DualShockers/Player.One Reviews)
The most praised postgame elements across 8 ROM hacks reviewed:
1. Strong trainers scattered in the world (rematches, hidden bosses)
2. Legendary encounter chains with narrative context
3. Side quests that revisit familiar areas with new context
4. Quick team-building tools so players can try postgame battles efficiently

Legends of Hoenn already has #2 and #3. Missing: hidden boss fights (#1) and team-building QoL (#4).
