# Postgame Player Priorities: What Makes ROM Hack Endgame Worth Playing

**Cycle**: 50 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Findings

The most celebrated ROM hack postgames (Unbound, Reborn, Radical Red) share a pattern: **battle content is woven into narrative or mission structure**, not presented as bare rematch ladders.

### What players value most (ranked by community consensus):
1. **Competitive-level boss AI** — EVs, held items, smart switching
2. **Legendary hunts** — roaming or fixed encounters as chase goals
3. **Side quests / missions** — give context and rewards to postgame battles
4. **Battle facilities** — Battle Frontier or similar endgame challenges
5. **Narrative closure** — Reborn's postgame gives every NPC their own chapter; players love this

### Critical insight: Rematch tiers alone feel hollow
Pure "fight the E4 again but stronger" without context is the weakest form of postgame. Top hacks frame rematches within a story beat:
- Radical Red sends you to Sevii Islands with new rivals
- Reborn ties postgame battles to character arc conclusions
- Unbound wraps rematches in a mission system with rewards

### For LoH specifically:
The migration narrative is a natural frame for rematch escalation — "the migration intensifies, E4 adapts their teams." But without new areas or side quests (hard on vanilla pokeemerald), **narrative NPCs and dialogue polish** are the best available tools to make rematches feel motivated rather than arbitrary.
