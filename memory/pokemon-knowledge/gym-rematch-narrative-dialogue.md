# Gym Rematch Narrative Dialogue Design

**Cycle**: 103 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## What Top Hacks Do

### Radical Red
- Rematch triggers are narrative — NPC tells you gym leaders want a rematch after a story milestone (defeating Giovanni at Silph Co.)
- Erika's rematch requires completing a side quest (healing her sick Gloom) before she'll fight — ties narrative to gameplay
- Rematches aren't just harder fights; they're story beats

### Inclement Emerald / Blazing Emerald
- Rematch teams are meticulously crafted with EVs, items, movesets
- Dialogue itself is less documented — the mechanical design carries the weight
- Community focuses more on team quality than dialogue quality

### Community Consensus (PokeCommunity)
- **Character bios first**: Define personality, backstory, speech patterns BEFORE writing dialogue (DJTiKi guide)
- **Consistency**: All dialogue should follow same syntax and tone conventions
- **Distinct voices**: Each gym leader should sound different — type specialty shapes personality
- **Capitalize**: Moves, Items, Abilities, Pokémon names, Area names (PokéCommunity Daily standard)

## Key Principles for LoH Gym Rematch Dialogue

1. **Reference the migration**: Each leader should acknowledge foreign species appearing in their domain — not generically but specifically ("I've been training with a Houndoom that wandered into the volcano")
2. **Show growth**: Tier 1 → Tier 4 dialogue should show the leader evolving as a trainer, not just "let's battle again"
3. **Keep it short**: 2-3 lines max per dialogue box on GBA. One personality line + one team/migration reference is enough
4. **Tie to team composition**: If Norman's Tier 4 has Ursaring, his dialogue should foreshadow it ("The migration brought fierce creatures from distant forests...")
5. **Side-quest hooks** (Radical Red pattern): Even a one-line hint about WHERE a migration species can be found rewards attentive players
