## C356 — v3.2 T1 "ring in the ash": silent persistent traces (searched Nov 2026)
- **Silent worn-path traces mostly go unnoticed.** Death Stranding's player-worn trails (a faint trail becomes a path with use; Sony patent "gradual building of a route") were "a secret feature most players missed" in DS1 (Gfinity, 2025). Even a AAA trace with no text was missed by most players. T1 is a bonus for observant players. Don't count it as proof the arc landed. Measure it only with an open recall question ("did anything on Route 113 look different on your second visit?"), never by naming the ring.
- **Indexical storytelling** (Fernández-Vara, GDC 2012 / DiGRA): a trace reads as story only when the player can tie the effect to a cause they remember. The DiGRA paper notes players mostly pick traces up in passing and seldom search like "archaeologists". So the ring must be on screen the moment the player arrives, with nothing to find. Its value is that it was there before the player stepped. The checklist step should check it on the first frame after the warp, before the player walks: the player's own fresh steps leave the same 0x212 and would hide it.
- No public pokeemerald doc covers setmetatile persistence. The pret wiki only says metatile changes are "most common during map load" and that `wait*` commands in level scripts hardlock. That confirms the ON_LOAD choice and rules out any waitstate/delay in Route113_OnLoad.

## C354 — check_beat_visibility lint + "beside the path" placement (searched Nov 2026; thin)
- **No public pokeemerald event/visibility lint exists.** Closest is Porygon's `validate_scripts` (an MCP skill, not CI): it flags **dangling labels** (map.json `script` names with no `Label::` in scripts.inc) and undefined FLAG_/VAR_/ITEM_ constants, because a typo'd label silently breaks the event while an undefined constant fails the build. Worth folding into check_beat_visibility: each beat object's `script` and each hide-flag OnTransition label must resolve. Poryscript documents adding checks to `.github/workflows/build.yml`; LoH's `check_all` already covers that.
- **Critical vs golden path** (Angry GM / level-design writing): the critical path is the shortest route; the golden path is the designer's preferred route, which may wander. Off-path content should be "rewarded, not required". A Portsmouth study finds players read the path mainly from **layout and lighting**, so a beat one row off a flat sand walk is seen; one around a rock or behind a trainer's sightline is not. A lint can't measure this. GBA view (240x160 px = 15x10 metatiles, player near centre) means "within ~4 rows / 7 columns of a BFS walk tile" is a cheap static proxy for "on screen while walking past".
- Silent playtests that log missed content are the standard fix for discoverability. Static lints only stand in for that until a tester runs the game (C355 milestone).

## C353 — Post-v3.1 planning: leaving the "…?" open, luring players to a quiet beat (searched Nov 2026; opinion-grade)
- **Ambiguity holds up only when it's optional and the player still gets a payoff.** Game Developer's Dear Esther piece: the open story was praised, but players who *invested in interpreting* it were "frustrated or offended" to find there was no answer. Wayline: unresolved ambiguity that "seems arbitrary" reads as being cheated. The Witness (Blow): hidden layers reward only the players who dig, and the core stays complete. Rule for the page-3 "…?": keep it open (C347), but never make the doubt look like it asks for an action, e.g. a new lead, item or location. An open doubt that sends the player searching becomes Dear Esther's "no solution" trap.
- **Vanilla Gen III signposting by TV outbreak is random and gets missed.** Outbreaks reach the TV through record mixing/daily rolls (Bulbapedia Mass outbreak; Smogon Gen 3 swarms thread). A PokéBase player saw none in about two months. So pointing players to Route 106 with a random TV/Match Call hint repeats the vanilla failure. A pointer must be deterministic: a fixed NPC line or a place the player already passes.
- **Scene split, 2026** (PokéCommunity "future of romhacks", 468 FR/EM hacks since 2015): Story/Region (Unbound-like) vs Difficulty/QoL (Radical Red-like). r/PokemonROMhacks Jan 2026 "best 2025 story" thread praised *Odyssey* for narrative + **pacing**. A May 2026 dev post (*Ambrosia* HP/damage display) drew mixed feedback, which shows info-heavy UI is contested. LoH sits in the story camp, where pacing is what gets praised, not volume.
- Sources: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/ambiguity-in-game-design-what-developers-can-learn-from-dear-esther , https://www.wayline.io/blog/ambiguous-endings-in-games , https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-for-a-sense-of-mystery-and-wonder , https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mass_outbreak , https://pokemondb.net/pokebase/86975/when-can-i-expect-an-outbreak-in-pokemon-emerald-gba , https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/the-future-of-pok%C3%A9mon-romhacks.536951/

## C350 — Beat C hail in the ash: what Gen III hail actually does (searched Nov 2026)
- **Gen III hail is chip damage only**: 1/16 max HP per turn to non-Ice/non-immune mons, 5 turns (Bulbapedia Hail move page; its Blizzard sure-hit clause sits under "Generation IV to VIII"). Ice Body / Snow Cloak are Gen IV (Uranium wiki). No Def boost (Inclement Emerald adds a 1.5x Ice Def boost + "Whiteout" ability precisely because vanilla hail is weak; unverified Scribd doc).
- **Players WRONGLY expect Blizzard to be sure-hit in Emerald hail**: PokéBase thread "Am I lucky to have never missed using Hail Blizzard in Emerald?" — answer: still 70%. Smogon ADV posters confirm. Same trap as C349 Lightning Rod: never write a line implying hail helps her Blizzard; a Blizzard miss right after Hail reads as a bug.
- **The true, sayable rule**: Ice types take no hail damage. "Hail can't touch my foxes" is honest whether or not hail goes up, and promises no accuracy.
- **No source on vanilla Gen III AI picking weather turn 1** (expansion docs describe an AI that prioritizes field setup; vanilla doesn't). Treat the opener as probabilistic.
- Sources: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hail_(move) , https://pokemondb.net/pokebase/418598/am-lucky-to-have-never-missed-using-hail-blizzard-in-emerald , https://www.smogon.com/forums/goto/post?id=3150849 , https://pokemon-uranium.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Body_(ability) , https://www.scribd.com/document/541056040/Battle-Changes , https://expansion-docsite-kildemal.pages.dev/tutorials/ai_flags

## C349 — Beat B storm-chaser: Lightning Rod, Parasol Lady, rain teams (searched Nov 2026)
- **Gen III Lightning Rod = redirect only, doubles only**: Bulbapedia + veekun: before Gen V it gives no Electric immunity and no SpA boost; it only pulls foes' Electric moves to the holder. In a singles fight Ludicolo_H's slot-0 LIGHTNING_ROD does NOTHING. Pokestop/BreezeWiki fan pages wrongly describe Gen V behaviour, so players will expect immunity. Don't write any line that hints the ace "eats" Electric moves.
- **Parasol Lady is the canonical rain-setter**: Bulbapedia trainer-class page says they mostly use Water types and often carry weather moves like Rain Dance. The class choice reads right with no explanation.
- **Inclement Emerald precedent** (unofficial Scribd battle-changes doc, unverified): permanent field rain is nerfed (1.2x) while Rain Dance rain stays 1.5x. Big hacks treat always-on route weather as too strong. LoH's scripted rain is a single fight, so vanilla 1.5x is fine. But Thunder never missing on 3 mons is the whole threat; keep the levels below the Reckoning bosses.
- No public reviews of a "trainer summons the weather" overworld moment were found; this beat is ahead of the documented scene.

## C348 — Encounter-odds lint + the empty roost (searched Nov 2026; thin)
- **No public validator for pokeemerald `wild_encounters.json` turned up** (searches: pret wiki, PokéCommunity). `make check_encounter_odds` has no outside model to copy. Two facts from the community for its design: (1) slot % is fixed by slot INDEX in the engine (pret pokered wiki "Edit the wild encounters"; Emerald land = 20/20/10/10/10/10/5/5/4/4/1/1), not stored per entry, so the lint must derive % from index and sum duplicate-species slots; (2) the JSON is regenerated through an Inja template (`wild_encounters.json.txt`, PokéCommunity 496361), so the lint reads the JSON source, not the generated .h.
- **The per-map `encounter_rate` also counts**: a 10% slot on a map with a low rate still feels rare. Print each floor map's encounter_rate next to the % (informational; don't fail on it).
- **Big hacks fix rare finds with a tool, not data**: Radical Red's DexNav (register a seen mon, then search it) is how it handles hard-to-find species (GameBrew, SA LP thread). LoH has no DexNav, so data floors are its stand-in. Keep that as the rationale in the lint header.
- **Environmental storytelling**: players read meaning into placed objects and often invent more than the designer meant (Game Developer, "Environmental storytelling"). The roost works best seen BEFORE the one line of text. Don't add a sign or second box.
- Sources: https://github.com/pret/pokered/wiki/Edit-the-wild-encounters , https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/firered-safari-zone-enounters-not-showing-up-in-porymap.496361/latest , https://www.gamebrew.org/wiki/Pokemon_Radical_Red_GBA , https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/environmental-storytelling

## C347 — Weather / timed field events (condensed C364)
- Gen III field weather carries into battle from turn 1; `setweather` makes a weather beat cheap. Weather-gated spawns: copy the Essentials "Weather Encounters" override %, never a table swap. Mirage Tower sandstorm = vanilla "place stays changed". Legends: Arceus random distortions read as grind loops, while a scripted, once-told event is the one players remember. State any weather rule in one player-facing line.
- Sources: Bulbapedia Weather; PokéCommunity 429803, 497707, 444136; skaternutko/weather-mayhem.

## C364 — v3.3 "First Ink" 4-5: early fight / place register (searched Nov 2026; thin)
- **No hack found where an early gym fight or dialogue reacts to the player's caught mons / discoveries** (searches: PokéCommunity, Romhacking.net, r/PokemonROMhacks). Early-gym hacks change rosters, levels and status gimmicks (Emerald Encore, Limited Emerald, carsonic status mod), never what the player *knows*. LoH would be ahead of the documented scene, but there is no reception data to lean on.
- **Vanilla precedent already in the target map**: Emerald's Dewford Gym is pitch-black; the light radius widens with each trainer beaten and the whole gym lights when Brawly falls (Bulbapedia / Serebii / Thonky). That is a vanilla "a fight changes the place" beat in the Hours 3-6 window. A v3.3 fight beat can echo this grammar: the result shows in the room, not in a speech.
- Boss-design writing (Game Developer "3 elements", gamedesign blogs): a boss works as a *test of what the player already learned*, ideally with visible clues earlier; a fight "plopped down from nowhere" reads as arbitrary. For LoH: the fight should test something the red ink taught (e.g. the Changed form's real type), and the clue is the inked page itself.
