# v3.0 Polish Pass & v4.0 Direction Research

**Cycle**: 97 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Polish Pass: What Community Expects

From reviews of top-rated hacks (Unbound, Radical Red, Emerald Imperium, Emerald Squared):

1. **Consistency is the #1 polish signal** — no jarring contrast between polished routes and vanilla dungeons. Players notice dead zones immediately.
2. **Bug-free > feature-rich** — players abandon hacks with softlocks. A complete, stable v3.0 > a buggy v4.0.
3. **Every feature must feel complete** — half-baked systems hurt more than missing ones.

## v4.0 Direction: Gym Leader Rematches — Community Best Practices

From PokéCommunity threads (Emerald Azure, Emerald Squared, Re:Emerald, Emerald Imperium):

1. **Scaling rematch teams tied to story milestones** (Re:Emerald approach, ORAS-style) — widely praised over arbitrary triggers.
2. **Rematches as postgame gating** — Emerald Squared requires gym rematches before Battle Frontier access, giving players clear incentive.
3. **Thematic consistency in rematch teams** — no random off-types. Gym leaders should expand their specialty, not abandon it.
4. **Competitive-level AI for rematches** — EV-trained Pokémon with smart AI (Emerald Azure standard).
5. **Trainer party pools for boss variety** — randomized boss teams add replay value (Emerald Squared).
6. **No legendaries unless story-justified** — community strongly values fairness in difficulty.
7. **Optional boss trainers with rewards** — Emerald Imperium adds mini-bosses that incentivize exploration.

## Key Takeaway for LoH

LoH already has rematch tiers (4 tiers per gym leader). The v4.0 opportunity is making those rematches feel meaningful: tie them to postgame progression, ensure teams reflect the migration theme (gym leaders adapting to new species in Hoenn), and use them as gating for Battle Frontier or other postgame content.
