# Post-v1.2 Direction — What Elevates a Mature Emerald Hack

**Cycle**: 157 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### Difficulty Modes — The #1 Differentiator for Replayability
- **Unbound**: 4 modes (Vanilla, Difficult, Expert, Insane) — community consensus "most complete hack ever"
- **Emerald Enhanced**: 6 modes including Nuzlocke and Frontier-specific
- **Radical Red**: Hard by default, optional Hardcore/Minimal Grinding modes
- **Black Pearl Emerald**: Dedicated Nuzlocke mode built-in
- Implementation: typically changes trainer AI level, EVs/IVs on enemy teams, level caps, item restrictions

### Trade Evolution Alternatives — Universally Expected
- Every top-rated hack solves this (link cable item, level-up, or automatic on trade-evolution level)
- Legends of Hoenn has 6 added species but trade evos for vanilla mons (Alakazam, Machamp, Gengar, Golem) remain vanilla-locked

### Following Pokemon — The "Wow Factor"
- Emerald Seaglass and Black Pearl both ship this; generates immediate community hype
- Very complex to implement in vanilla decomp without expansion repo

### Full Dex Obtainability
- Players strongly prefer hacks where all Pokemon are catchable without trading
- Version exclusives and trade evos are the two friction points

### What Players Actually Recommend
- Polish > volume. Unbound is praised for completeness, not size
- QoL + difficulty options + postgame depth = the trifecta
- Narrative/story changes are appreciated but secondary to mechanical depth
