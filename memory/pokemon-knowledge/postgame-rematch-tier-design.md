# Postgame Rematch Tier Design: Community Best Practices

**Cycle**: 69 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Patterns from Top Hacks

### Pokémon Infinite Fusion — Randomized Pool + Progressive Unlock
- E4 rematches draw from a **random pool** of Pokémon, growing as player progresses postgame
- Player **chooses tier difficulty** when starting the challenge
- Higher tiers unlock sequentially — must beat prior tier first

### Pokémon Elite Redux — Crafted Variety + Access QoL
- Up to **4 crafted rematches per trainer** (E4 + gym leaders)
- No day-wait for rematches — battle anytime
- Singles or Doubles choice, with/without Legendaries
- Every rematch **individually crafted** — not just level scaling
- All parties consider new abilities/moves from engine changes

### Refined Platinum — Superboss Layer
- E4 rematches + **superboss questline** (Oak, Cyrus, Saturn, Frontier Brains)
- Superbosses unlocked sequentially via coherent quest
- Adds narrative motivation beyond "fight stronger teams"

## Design Principles for LoH Tiers 3-4
- Each tier should feel **meaningfully different** — not just +5 levels. New strategies, new species, different team compositions
- Consider giving tiers a **narrative hook** (Tier 3: "migration intensifies," Tier 4: "legendary-tier threats")
- Garchomp (600 BST) on Drake's Tier 3-4 teams is the obvious payoff for completing the Gible line
- With P/S split and Fairy type already implemented, tiers 3-4 can showcase these mechanics at their peak
