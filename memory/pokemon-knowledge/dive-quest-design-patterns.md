# Dive Quest & First Side Quest Design Patterns

**Cycle**: 193 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### Unbound's Dive = Postgame-Only
Unbound restricts Dive to postgame (obtained after becoming Champion). Underwater areas on Routes 14, 16, 17 + Ruins of Void feel special and exclusive. This validates the Elder's Current design: a dive-gated quest that only exists postgame inherently feels like "Champion-level content."

### First Quest Sets the Template
Unbound has 84 missions with diverse rewards (items, rare Pokémon, feature upgrades, capture rate boosts). Reviews consistently praise the variety. The first quest a player encounters defines their expectations for ALL side content. If it delivers atmosphere + meaningful reward, players seek the rest. If it's a generic fetch quest, they skip side content entirely.

### "Pleasantly Weird" Wins
One Unbound review specifically praises quests that were "pleasantly weird" — subverting expectations. The Elder's Current has this quality: reversed ocean currents → volcanic thermal vents → fossilized ghost coral is a genuinely surprising chain of causality that rewards investigation.

### Pokémon Pisces — Underwater as Core Theme
Pokémon Pisces (2025-2026) builds its entire identity around underwater exploration with custom water-based Pokémon forms. Shows community appetite for underwater content when done with care — not just as a transit corridor but as a destination.

### Quest Reward Diversity Matters
Unbound's mission rewards aren't just Pokémon — they include exclusive items, feature upgrades, and unique encounters. The Elder's Current rewarding both a regional form (Hoennian Corsola) AND Mystic Water follows this pattern of layered rewards.
