# Changed Trainer Witness Patterns — NPC Reactions to Player Transformation

**Cycle**: 235 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: Visual Acknowledgment Is Rare and Differentiated

Most ROM hacks with character customization (Crystal Clear, Radical Red) treat appearance as purely cosmetic — NPCs never comment on it. The "world notices your visual change" pattern is almost nonexistent in the Emerald hack space, making LoH's approach genuinely novel.

Where hacks DO have NPCs react to player changes, they use story-progression flags (Unbound's mission system, day/night shifts), not visual appearance triggers. LoH's approach — key relationship NPCs noticing a palette shift tied to the migration narrative — blends both: the flag is story-driven, the payoff is visual.

## Risk: The "Mirror" Anti-Pattern

The biggest pitfall: NPCs who just say "you look different" read as tutorial prompts, not worldbuilding. In mainline games (Legends Arceus), appearance-reactive dialogue works because NPCs comment through their own lens — a merchant notices your outfit because they sell clothes, not because they're a mirror.

**Applied to LoH**: Mom should notice through a mother's worry ("something in your eyes"), Norman through a gym leader's assessment ("you carry yourself differently"), Rival through competitive framing ("what happened to you out there?"). Each witness should filter the observation through their own relationship to the player.

## Pokemon Ambrosia's Rival Mirror

Pokemon Ambrosia (2024 GBC hack) features a rival who copies the player's exact party — a different kind of "witness" where the rival literally mirrors the player. While mechanically different from LoH's approach, it shows community appetite for rivals who are deeply connected to the player's journey rather than following an independent script.
