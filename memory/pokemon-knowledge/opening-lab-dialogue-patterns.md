# Opening Lab Dialogue Patterns in ROM Hacks

**Cycle**: 180 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### The Professor Speech Is the Hack's Mission Statement

The professor's dialogue after giving the Pokédex is the single most-read piece of custom text in any Emerald hack. Every player sees it exactly once, at full attention. Community consensus (PokéCommunity, r/PokemonROMhacks):

- **Keep it to 2-3 text boxes max.** Players are eager to start playing. A 5-box lecture will be skimmed.
- **Name concrete species or locations.** "Unusual POKéMON from other regions" is vague. "I've spotted a RIOLU near RUSTBORO" is memorable and gives the player a goal.
- **Match the professor's established personality.** Birch is a field researcher — he should sound excited and curious, not alarmed or lecturing. He runs into tall grass without thinking. His dialogue should feel spontaneous, not scripted.

### Anti-Patterns From Community Feedback

1. **Exposition dump**: Professor explains the entire hack premise in one monologue. Players tune out.
2. **Fourth-wall breaking**: "This ROM hack features difficulty modes!" Immersion-breaking.
3. **Inconsistent tone**: Professor suddenly sounds serious when the scene was casual.
4. **Too many text boxes**: 4+ boxes for a single NPC in the opening area is a red flag.

### Littleroot NPC Dialogue — Less Is More

PokéCommunity's "Newbie Mistakes" article emphasizes: flavor NPCs work best at 1-2 text boxes. The kid near the grass and the girl near the lab are flavor NPCs. Their job is to hint, not explain.

**Effective pattern**: Each NPC references the migration through their own lens (a kid heard a weird cry, a girl notices the professor's late nights). This is the Unbound/Gaia approach — same phenomenon, different perspectives.

### Formatting Conventions (pokeemerald)

- Proper nouns capitalized: POKéMON, HOENN, PROFESSOR BIRCH
- Species names in ALL CAPS: RIOLU, VULPIX
- 2-3 text boxes per NPC interaction
- Consistent color tags for gender-specific NPCs
