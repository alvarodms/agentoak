# Glacia Team Design Patterns — Froslass & Mamoswine in Emerald Hacks

**Cycle**: 213 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## How Top Hacks Handle Glacia with Cross-Gen Evos

**Inclement Emerald** (the community benchmark for difficulty hacks) gives Froslass and Mamoswine **completely different combat roles** on Glacia's rematch team:
- **Froslass**: Light Clay, Freeze-Dry + Psychic — hazard/screen setter, fast and fragile
- **Mamoswine**: Assault Vest, Icicle Crash + Earthquake — physical wallbreaker, bulky attacker
- Also has Abomasnow (Choice Scarf, Blizzard + Earth Power) and Walrein (Leftovers, Toxic + Aurora Veil)
- Key design: each Ice-type fills a distinct tactical niche — no redundancy

**Blazing Emerald** took a radical approach: removed Glacia entirely, replacing her with Bede (Fairy). This signals that vanilla Glacia's "double Glalie + double Sealeo" problem is widely recognized as weak design.

**Modern Emerald**: Includes both Froslass and Mamoswine but keeps simpler team structures.

## Evolution Methods Across Hacks

| Species | Canon Method | Modern Emerald | Royal Emerald | LoH Pattern |
|---------|-------------|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| Froslass | Dawn Stone (female Snorunt) | Female Snorunt Lv42 (morning) | Snorunt Lv42 | EVO_LEVEL likely |
| Mamoswine | Level up knowing Ancient Power | Ancient Power (move-based) | Piloswine Lv48 | EVO_LEVEL likely |

**Important**: Mamoswine is NOT a trade evo — it's a move-based evo in canon. LoH's EVO_LEVEL pattern covers trade evos cleanly, but Mamoswine requires a deliberate choice: level-based (simpler, consistent with LoH pattern) or move-based (if EVO_MOVE is supported). Most simpler hacks use level-based; competitive-focused hacks keep the move requirement.
