# Early Game Reset — Encounter Design Lessons

**Cycle**: 164 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### What Works in Early Routes
- **NFE-only rule**: Top hacks enforce "no fully evolved Pokemon before Badge 1." Legends of Hoenn currently violates this with Breloom (BST 460), Alakazam (BST 490), Heracross (BST 500) in Petalburg Woods at Lv 10-16.
- **Type diversity across starters**: If all early encounters share a weakness, one starter trivializes while another struggles. The v1.4 design addresses this well with varied types per route.
- **1% rare encounters generate genuine excitement**: Eevee at 1% on Route 101 is the right call — players report spending 30+ minutes hunting rare encounters in hacks, and the payoff feels earned. Eevee (BST 325) is appropriately weak early but exciting for its evolution potential.
- **Dratini at 40% destroys rarity psychology**: Current R101 having Dratini at 40% makes pseudo-legendaries feel like trash mobs. Moving to Safari Zone/R119 fishing restores the "event" feeling.

### Pitfalls to Avoid
- **Don't over-correct to boring**: Some vanilla-faithful hacks swing too far — if early routes are *only* Zigzagoon/Poochyena/Wurmple, players feel no pull to explore. The v1.4 design correctly adds Ralts (4%), Surskit, Marill to keep discovery alive.
- **Level mismatches**: Wild Pokemon faster than the player's team at Lv 2-3 can trap players who can't flee. Keep early rare encounters at appropriate levels (Eevee at Lv 4 is fine).
- **Adjacent route overlap**: No two adjacent routes sharing more than 1 top-3 species is a strong rule from the design doc.

### Stat Anchors
- Eevee: BST 325 (appropriate early catch, evolves to ~525 BST)
- Heracross: BST 500, 125 Atk (should be 1% in Petalburg Woods, not common)
- Dratini: BST 300 (deceptively weak early, but Dragonite at 600 is the reward — belongs mid/late game)
