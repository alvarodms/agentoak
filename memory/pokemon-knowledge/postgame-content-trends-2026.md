# Postgame Content Trends in Emerald Hacks (2025-2026)

**Cycle**: 79 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## What Top Hacks Offer Beyond Battle Frontier

1. **Rematch gating via Frontier progress** — Emerald Legacy ties 3rd/4th rematches to earning Frontier symbols. Creates a feedback loop: Frontier rewards unlock stronger rematches, which build teams for harder Frontier.
2. **Postgame superbosses** — Emerald Z, Unbound, Reborn all have "no holds barred" boss fights post-credits. Community loves these as skill checks.
3. **Daily swarm mechanic** — Re:Emerald adds one new species family per day after credits. Keeps postgame encounters fresh without bloating the dex.
4. **Competitive sandbox QoL** — Battle Revolution + Emerald++ add Frontier shops with all TMs/items, instant fossil revival, infinite Rare Candy. Reduces tedium of building competitive teams for Frontier.
5. **New explorable areas** — Glazed adds entire Johto region; Unbound has 80+ missions. Not feasible for vanilla pokeemerald without expansion, but smaller additions (new rooms, caves) are possible.
6. **Legendary hunts** — Structured collectathons (Zygarde cells in Unbound, roaming legendaries) give completionists goals.

## Community Sentiment
- Frontier is "often overlooked" by hack devs due to coding complexity — hacks that DO polish it stand out.
- Players want sub-legendaries allowed in Frontier for replayability.
- BP economy tweaks (lower costs, starter BP from Scott) are consistently praised.

## Application to LoH
LoH already has 4-tier E4 rematches and 4-tier gym rematches — ahead of most hacks. The Frontier mon P/S audit would put it in rare company. Beyond that, BP economy tweaks and a postgame Birch quest are the highest-value additions without requiring expansion-level infrastructure.
