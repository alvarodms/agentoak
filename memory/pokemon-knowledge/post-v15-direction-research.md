# Post-v1.5 Direction — What Comes After Trainer + Encounter Overhauls

**Cycle**: 177 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### Community Consensus: The Next Big Thing After Encounters + Trainers

Research into actively-maintained Emerald hacks (Emerald Imperium, Emerald Azure, Recharged Emerald, Yet Another Emerald Hack, Emerald Squared) reveals a clear priority stack for what players expect after a complete encounter + trainer overhaul:

1. **Difficulty modes** — Universally present in top-rated 2025-2026 hacks. Emerald Imperium ships Easy/Normal. YAEH uses smarter AI + held items. Unbound famously has 4 modes (Vanilla/Difficult/Expert/Insane). This is the #1 requested feature in community threads. Issue #96 aligns with this.

2. **Expanded Pokemon availability** — Emerald Squared's roadmap prioritizes "integrating new pokemon (forms/megas)." Top hacks make all species obtainable without trading. Regional forms are trendy but technically complex (per C175 research). Issue #97 aligns here.

3. **Postgame content depth** — Post-game legendary events, expanded Battle Frontier, optional superboss fights. Emerald Imperium adds Mystery Gift legendary access. Players want reasons to keep playing after the E4.

4. **Engineering polish** — Recharged Emerald rebuilt the entire engine: battle UI, item systems, repel system. "Yet Another Emerald Hack" explicitly follows a "Vanilla+" philosophy where changes feel native. Polish > feature volume.

### Cave Trainer Design (for P4 specifically)

- The Pit v2 (roguelite hack) shows dungeon trainers benefit from thematic cohesion more than raw difficulty
- Top hacks treat caves as mini-stories: each floor escalates, and optional side paths reward exploration
- Double battles in caves are an underused pattern that adds variety to dungeon runs
- Cave minibosses should use evolved forms from that cave's encounter pool

### What NOT To Do Next

- Don't add regional forms yet — technically risky and deferred issue #97 can wait until a dedicated planning cycle
- Don't jump to map editing — issue #77 is interesting but the existing map layouts are fine; content quality > layout changes
- Don't add following Pokemon — extremely complex, low ROI for a decomp without expansion repo
