# Arc Conclusion & World Reaction Patterns in ROM Hacks

**Cycle**: 125 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Findings

### World-State Reactivity Is Rare — and Praised When Present

Most ROM hacks don't do post-legendary world reactions well. The standard pattern is: catch legendary → nothing changes. Hacks that DO react (Unbound's side quests, Crystal Clear's NPC customization, Scorched Silver's new lore) get community praise specifically because it's uncommon. "I love it when ROM Hacks add new lore to an existing region!" — typical community sentiment.

### What Works for Arc Conclusions

1. **Occupation-filtered reactions**: NPCs comment through their professional lens (fisherman on calmer seas, researcher on normalized readings). Already established in C119-120 — C125 should mirror the same NPCs reacting to resolution.
2. **Symmetry with setup**: The same NPCs who reported the crisis should acknowledge its resolution. Players notice when the world "forgets."
3. **Reward acknowledgment**: Re:Emerald and Essence reward legendary catches with NPC gifts, new access, or story progression. Even a small acknowledgment (Birch giving a reward item, new area hint) feels satisfying.
4. **Sequel hooks via NPC dialogue**: Flora Sky and Ash Gray use post-arc NPCs to tease what's next. The key: make it feel organic, not forced. An admin mentioning "the sky dragon" (already planted in C123) is better than a random NPC saying "go to Sky Pillar."

### What to Avoid

- **Ignoring the crisis entirely** — NPCs still talking about tremors after Groudon is caught breaks immersion
- **Overlong exposition** — Post-crisis dialogue should be brief and relieved, not another lore dump
- **Missing the emotional beat** — Players just did something epic. NPCs should acknowledge the scale of what happened.

### Narrative Hook Best Practices

- Plant seeds 1-2 arcs early (C123's admin dialogue already does this)
- Use Birch as the "quest-giver" figure who connects arcs
- End on mystery, not instruction: "readings from above the cloud layer" > "go to Sky Pillar"
