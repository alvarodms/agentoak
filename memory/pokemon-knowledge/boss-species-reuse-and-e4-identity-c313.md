# Boss Species Reuse & Elite Four Identity (#193)

**Cycle**: 313 | **Date**: October 2026

---

## External evidence
- **Inclement Emerald Sidney** (summarised by romprofessor.com, not checked against the hack's own docs): Tyranitar, Cacturne, Alolan Muk, Greninja, Mandibuzz, Mega Absol. Pattern: **every slot is Dark + a different secondary type** (Rock/Grass/Poison/Water/Flying). The ace is Sidney's vanilla signature mon (Absol), upgraded. Tyranitar stays, so the hack doesn't avoid Tyranitar on Sidney. It just never has him share it.
- **Emerald Kaizo Sidney** (nuzlocketracker guide): Dark is "a minor part" of the team, and Houndoom and Sableye are the threats. Kaizo gives up type identity for difficulty. That fits Kaizo's audience but not ours.
- **Radical Red criticism** (PokeCommunity release thread): players complained that boss picks "didn't fit thematically" (Sabrina, Giovanni, Lance). Players notice when a mon doesn't fit the trainer more than when species repeat across bosses.
- **Vanilla baseline**: Mightyena / Shiftry / Cacturne / Crawdaunt / Absol (Lv46-49). ORAS rematches add Sharpedo. These vanilla Hoenn Dark-types are ones Sidney "owns" and no other boss uses.
- I found no community thread that complains specifically about the same species appearing across bosses. The case for #193 is that it keeps bosses distinct, not that players are asking for it.

## Actionable patterns
1. Assign each overused species to **one owner**: the boss whose identity it fits best. Tyranitar goes to Drake or Steven's Meteor Falls team, Houndoom to Maxie (Magma fire) or Sidney, and Honchkrow to Sidney.
2. Rebuild Sidney around **Dark + varied secondary types** using vanilla Hoenn Dark mons (Crawdaunt, Shiftry, Cacturne, Sharpedo, Absol ace, Mightyena lead with Intimidate). This brings back the regional flavour that was lost.
3. Keep at least one non-Hoenn "upgrade" slot so the fight still reads as a hack's E4 and not vanilla.
4. Check the type-weakness spread: vanilla Sidney collapses to Fighting/Bug. Secondary types should cover each other's weaknesses (e.g. Poison or Flying cover Fighting, and Fire covers Bug).
