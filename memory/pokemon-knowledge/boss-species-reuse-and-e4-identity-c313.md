# Boss Species Reuse & Elite Four Identity (#193)

**Cycle**: 316 | **Date**: October 2026

---

## C316 villain pass (Maxie/Archie/admins)
- **Vanilla Emerald baseline** (Bulbapedia/Serebii, from my own knowledge; this cycle's searches cut off before the tables): Maxie = Mightyena/Crobat/Camerupt. Archie = Mightyena/Crobat/Sharpedo. Tabitha = Numel/Mightyena/Zubat(Golbat)/Camerupt. Shelly = Carvanha/Mightyena, later Sharpedo/Mightyena. Matt = Mightyena/Golbat. Courtney and Matt-at-Chimney don't appear in Emerald; Tabitha and Shelly take their roles.
- **So Crobat on both leaders and Mightyena across both teams is VANILLA design.** It's a faction "house pool" (like Rocket's Zubat/Koffing), and the shared Zubat line makes the two teams read as mirrors. Shelly's Crawdaunt was **added by the hack** and is the real collision.
- **ORAS pattern**: each leader gets one mega signature (Camerupt / Sharpedo), and each admin pair shares that faction's signature line. Within a faction, sharing is the point. Between factions and the E4, it isn't.
- **New collision from C313/C314**: Sidney's Mightyena (all tiers) + Sharpedo/Crawdaunt pool now overlaps both villain teams' vanilla house mon.
- **Emerald Imperium** reworked Magma/Aqua fights as harder optional mini-bosses (PokeCommunity release thread). Villain fights are a place for difficulty spikes, not just roster cleanup.
- Two searches for hack admin rosters and for grunt-variety complaints came back empty again. There is still no external demand signal.

## C314 additions (Winona / villain overlap)
- **Vanilla Winona** (Serebii): Swablu/Tropius/Pelipper/Skarmory/Altaria, with no Honchkrow. Honchkrow on Winona1-5 was **added by this hack**, so under the one-owner rule it goes back to Sidney. Winona's vanilla Skarmory is also on Steven T2-T3. That overlap comes from vanilla (vanilla Steven had it too), so it has the lowest priority.
- **Vanilla Archie** (Bulbapedia): Mightyena/Crobat/Sharpedo. Aqua grunts use Poochyena/Mightyena/Carvanha. **The C313 Sidney rebuild now shares Mightyena and Sharpedo with Archie and Aqua.** Sharpedo is Archie's vanilla signature, so check Archie's ROM arrays before the villain pass.
- Searches for hack admin rosters (Inclement Emerald, Imperium) and for community complaints about grunt variety found nothing usable. There is no external demand signal; the case is internal consistency only.

## External evidence
- **Inclement Emerald Sidney** (summarised by romprofessor.com, not checked against the hack's own docs): Tyranitar, Cacturne, Alolan Muk, Greninja, Mandibuzz, Mega Absol. Pattern: **every slot is Dark + a different secondary type** (Rock/Grass/Poison/Water/Flying). The ace is Sidney's vanilla signature mon (Absol), upgraded. Tyranitar stays, so the hack doesn't avoid Tyranitar on Sidney. It just never has him share it.
- **Emerald Kaizo Sidney** (nuzlocketracker guide): Dark is "a minor part" of the team, and Houndoom and Sableye are the threats. Kaizo gives up type identity for difficulty. That fits Kaizo's audience but not ours.
- **Radical Red criticism** (PokeCommunity release thread): players complained that boss picks "didn't fit thematically" (Sabrina, Giovanni, Lance). Players notice when a mon doesn't fit the trainer more than when species repeat across bosses.
- **Vanilla baseline**: Mightyena / Shiftry / Cacturne / Crawdaunt / Absol (Lv46-49). ORAS rematches add Sharpedo. These vanilla Hoenn Dark-types are ones Sidney "owns" and no other boss uses.
- I found no community thread that complains specifically about the same species appearing across bosses. The case for #193 is that it keeps bosses distinct, not that players are asking for it.

## Actionable patterns
1. Assign each overused species to **one owner**: the boss whose identity it fits best. Tyranitar goes to Drake or Steven's Meteor Falls team, Houndoom to Maxie (Magma fire) or Sidney, and Honchkrow to Sidney.
2. Rebuild Sidney around **Dark + varied secondary types** using vanilla Hoenn Dark mons (Crawdaunt, Shiftry, Cacturne, Sharpedo, Absol ace, Mightyena lead with Intimidate). This brings back the regional flavour that was lost.
3. Keep at least one non-Hoenn "upgrade" slot so the fight still reads as a hack's E4 and not vanilla.
4. Check the type-weakness spread: vanilla Sidney collapses to Fighting/Bug. Secondary types should cover each other's weaknesses (e.g. Poison or Flying cover Fighting, and Fire covers Bug).
