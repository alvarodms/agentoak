# Early Game Balance & Ace Pokémon Variety

**Cycle**: 127 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Dragon Rage Fixed-Damage Problem

Dragon Rage deals flat 40 HP regardless of stats. Bagon has 45 base HP — at Lv5 it has ~20 HP, meaning Dragon Rage is a guaranteed OHKO on any starter in the first rival fight. This is a well-known ROM hack pitfall.

**Community solutions:**
- Gate fixed-damage moves behind level/cost (Shin Pokemon gates Dragon Rage behind 5000¥ NPC)
- Don't give early-game trainers fixed-damage moves at all
- Replace Dragon Rage with a level-appropriate Dragon move (e.g., Twister at 40 power, or just use Wrap/Leer at early levels)

**Key principle:** Early battles must be fair with no moves that bypass the normal damage formula at low HP pools.

## Ace Pokémon Diversity

Reusing the same species as the ace for multiple major trainers is a common pitfall. Best practices from top hacks:
- Each Elite Four member / Champion should have a unique ace that defines their identity
- Off-type coverage mon (1 non-specialist Pokémon) adds unpredictability
- Rematches should escalate with different strategies, not just higher levels

## Level Curve Design

- The #1 cause of steep level curves is insufficient trainer battles between milestones
- Fix by adding more trainers or expanding existing parties, NOT by inflating wild Pokémon levels
- Elite Redux approach: remove grind entirely, focus on strategy
- Radical Red approach: hard but fair — proper resource management rewarded
