## Narrative Hack Differentiation in 2026

Unbound remains the gold standard for narrative ROM hacks in 2026. Its key differentiator: the war-torn history of Borrius isn't just flavor text — it manifests in damaged landscapes, distrustful NPCs, and side quests that reveal the region's painful past. LoH's migration narrative follows the same principle: the migration isn't a mechanic, it's a world state that changes everything. The Reckoning arc (v2.6) is exactly the kind of "consequences" storytelling that elevates Unbound above other hacks.

After consequences, the natural narrative progression is **resolution or new threat**. Unbound uses escalating reveals (each side quest deepens the war's legacy). LoH could follow this with Devon Corp (#167) as a "human consequences" arc — organizations that tried to exploit the migration.

## Custom Shinies — SparklingRed Case Study

SparklingRed (FireRed hack) ships 350+ custom shinies with 1/8 shiny odds — shinies ARE the hack's identity. Players responded positively ("I really dig the reworks"). But SparklingRed is built on this premise. For LoH, where identity is narrative + migration, custom shinies would be supplementary, not defining. The scope is massive (42 custom species × 2 palettes minimum) for a feature that doesn't serve the core narrative.

## Custom Abilities/Moves (Issues #151, #152)

C286 research stands: on vanilla pokeemerald, custom abilities cost 3-5 cycles each. Community reception of Radical Red and Inclement Emerald shows players value **ability reassignment** (existing abilities on unexpected species) more than custom creation. LoH already has Toxic Touch — strategic reassignment of Gen 4-9 abilities to regional forms gives the "custom feel" at 1/10th the cost.

## Postgame Trends

Players want meaningful postgame beyond Battle Frontier. Most hacks add difficulty — LoH's narrative postgame (Reckoning, Birch quests, emotional NPC arcs) is genuinely rare and differentiating. Deepening this is higher-value than adding battle content.
