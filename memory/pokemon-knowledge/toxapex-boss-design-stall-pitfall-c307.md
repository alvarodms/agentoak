# Toxapex on Boss Trainers — The Stall Pitfall

**Cycle**: 307 | **Date**: May 2026

---

## Key Finding: Radical Red Nerfed Toxapex

Radical Red — the most popular difficulty hack — removed Recover from Toxapex's learnset in v2.2. Community reaction on PokéCommunity: Toxapex became "a bit useless" for monotype runs after the nerf. Players noted that most bosses in RR rely on "speed and pure strength rather than bulky teams" — nobody remembered ever facing a stall team from a boss trainer.

**Why they nerfed it**: Toxapex's stat spread (50/63/152/53/142/35) makes it a nearly indestructible wall that can barely damage anything. On a boss trainer, this creates a tedious loop: Toxic → Recover → Protect → repeat. The player eventually wins but wastes 15+ turns. This is "difficult" in a way that feels like waiting, not strategizing.

## Toxapex Stats Context

- 152 Def / 142 SpD with only 50 HP — extremely high proportional bulk
- 63 Atk / 53 SpA — cannot meaningfully threaten opponents offensively
- 35 Speed — always moves last
- Ability: Merciless (crits on poisoned targets) gives it offensive identity IF poison is applied first

## Design Recommendation for Juan

If Toxapex gets Recover + Toxic on a boss trainer, it becomes a tedious stall wall. Instead:
- **Use Merciless aggressively**: Toxic Spikes from an earlier team member → Toxapex crits with Liquidation/Poison Jab
- **Skip Recover**: Give it Baneful Bunker / Toxic / Liquidation / Poison Jab or Venoshock
- **Or limit to rematch tiers only**: Where players have stronger teams and can break through faster
