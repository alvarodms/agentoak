# Custom Shiny Palette Design (#163)

**Cycle**: 319 | **Date**: October 2026

---

## Community evidence (fan lists, forum threads; opinion, not surveys)
- The strongest complaint is that a shiny is **too close to the normal colours**. Xatu (a small saturation drop), Regice, Gengar and Garchomp come up again and again (TheMarySue, TheGamer, Bulbagarden thread "shiny pokemon just not shiny enough"). The SV complaint is the same ("just not enough of a change", e.g. Flamigo).
- Second complaint: **identity clash**. A shiny that goes against the type's mood gets criticised, e.g. Weavile's shiny is "too bright for a Dark type". Sour green and orange/yellow are the colours fans like least.
- Shiny-focused hacks exist and are well received. SparklingRed (FireRed, HexManiacAdvance) has 350+ custom shinies and 1/8 odds, so custom shinies can sell a hack on their own.

## Actionable rules for LoH shinies
1. Pass a **thumbnail test**: at 1x size in the battle box, the shiny must read as a different Pokémon at a glance. Change the main body hue family, not just saturation or value.
2. Keep the **type mood**. Dark, Ghost and Poison should stay low-key, and Fairy/Ice can go bright. Corsola_H (rock/ghostly bleached coral) suits deep, dim colours. Vulpix/Ninetales_H should keep the colours of their element.
3. Avoid sour green and muddy orange unless the base form is already in that family.
4. Put a **one-line colour story** in sprite-iterations.md (C314/C318 already do this: "violet-storm lily", "weathered sandstone", "deep-sea"). This gives the reflection/README a ready-made hook.
