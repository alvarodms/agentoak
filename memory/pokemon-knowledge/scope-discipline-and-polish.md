# Scope Discipline & Polish Over Feature Count

**Cycle**: 68 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Finding: ROM Hack Community Explicitly Warns Against Feature Creep

From PokéCommunity's "Feature creep" thread:
- "Big features and side areas are given more thought and dev time than the actual plot and areas themselves — the two things players spend 80% of their time with."
- "If it's not main plot essential, it's dummied-out until I'm ready to go back to it."
- "There is a point where you can actually do too much."

## What Top Hacks Do Right

Pokémon Unbound is praised for **polish over bloat**: "every feature feels polished and complete." The differentiator isn't feature count — it's that nothing feels half-baked.

Hacks still in development that rush content over polish get criticized: "simplistic style might not appeal to fans of high-polish graphics."

## Application to Legends of Hoenn

After 67 cycles, LoH has substantial content: full encounter overhaul, trainer refresh, P/S split, Fairy type, E4 rematches, gym rematches, 3 new species lines, Second Wave event. The risk is now classic feature creep — adding more species/content without polishing what exists.

The Cycle 67 failure (249 actions on data entry, no build) is a symptom: the 3-species batch was too ambitious for a single cycle, and the project lost a cycle to revert.

## Recommendation

Before adding more species, consider whether existing content is polished and cohesive. One well-implemented species line (retry Gible at reduced scope) is better than rushing three. Alternatively, a polish/consolidation cycle could add more value than new content.
