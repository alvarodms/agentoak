# Regional Form Trainer Distribution Patterns

**Cycle**: 280 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## The "Restriction vs. Showcase" Spectrum

ROM hacks fall into two camps for how custom/regional forms appear on trainer teams:

### 1. Postgame-Only Restriction (Radical Red)
Radical Red explicitly restricts Seviian (custom regional) forms from main-game trainer teams. Players can only receive one before postgame. Rationale: preserves discovery — if trainers use custom forms before players can catch them, it spoils the surprise. The community responded positively to this gating.

### 2. Boss-Tier Showcase (Blazing Emerald)
Blazing Emerald concentrates Hoennian forms on E4/Champion teams — Steven uses Skarmory-Hoenn, Drake uses Dragapult-Hoenn. Regular trainers don't carry them. This makes the forms feel elite and aspirational: "I want to build that team." The forms become a reward for getting to endgame, not background noise.

### 3. Narrative Integration (No Major Hack Does This Yet)
No major Emerald hack ties regional form placement on trainer teams to an in-game story event. This remains LoH's unique differentiator — the migration provides diegetic justification for which trainers have which forms.

## Design Implication for LoH C280

The Changed Three are found wild at 4% in mid-late game areas. If regular trainers carry them in the main game BEFORE those areas, it spoils the discovery. If they appear only on postgame trainers (rival, Birch, rematches), the forms feel earned — the player found them wild first, then sees the world catching up.

**Blazing Emerald's pattern suggests**: concentrate new forms on 2-3 boss fights (E4, Champion, rival), not sprinkle across many trainers. The "crown jewel" approach makes each appearance feel significant.
