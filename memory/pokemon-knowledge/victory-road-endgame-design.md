# Victory Road & Endgame Trainer Design in ROM Hacks

**Cycle**: 174 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### What Top Hacks Do With Victory Road

1. **Victory Road as final exam** — Inclement Emerald and Radical Red treat Victory Road trainers as the last skill check before E4. Teams have 3-4 mons with full competitive sets, held items, and coverage moves. The difficulty gap between the last route and Victory Road is intentional — it signals "prepare yourself."

2. **Trainer class escalation** — Cooltrainers/Ace Trainers in Victory Road should feel like mini-bosses. In Radical Red, VR Ace Trainers run near-competitive sets (3-4 mons, held items, synergy). Regular trainers (Hikers, etc.) have simpler but still item-equipped teams.

3. **Species diversity matters** — Best hacks avoid repeating species across Victory Road trainers. Each trainer should introduce a different threat. PokeCommunity feedback consistently criticizes "wall of Geodudes" syndrome.

4. **Level curve purpose** — Victory Road trainers serve as EXP sources that bridge the gap between Route 128 (~Lv38-40) and E4 (~Lv44-58). Having ~15 trainers at Lv40-48 lets players organically reach E4-ready levels without grinding.

5. **Coverage move philosophy** — Every VR trainer should have at least one move that punishes common player team compositions. Ice Beam on Camerupt, Thunderbolt on Tentacruel, Shadow Ball on Dusclops — these force the player to think, not autopilot.
