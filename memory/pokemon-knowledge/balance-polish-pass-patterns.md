# Balance & Polish Pass Patterns — What Players Notice

**Cycle**: 294 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: "Fair Challenge" Is the #1 Player Differentiator

Players distinguish two kinds of difficulty:
1. **Smart difficulty** (praised): Better AI, competitive movesets, proper EV/IV spreads, strategic held items. Radical Red and Inclement Emerald set this standard.
2. **Stat inflation** (hated): Just raising trainer levels above the curve so the player is always underleveled. Dark Rising is the cautionary example.

The community consensus (PokeCommunity difficulty thread): the best balance comes from improving trainer AI and giving trainers proper EV/IV distribution + competitive movesets, while also giving the player tools to keep up (EV training spots, move tutors, etc.).

## Trainer Difficulty Tiers — What Top Hacks Do

- **Normal mode**: Trainers with 0 EVs, 0 IVs, basic movesets, no items. The "vanilla+ experience."
- **Challenge mode**: Trainers with full 252/252 EV spreads, 31 IVs, competitive movesets, held items (Leftovers, Choice items, Life Orb). Inclement Emerald's approach.
- **Key insight**: Just bumping IVs alone feels like generic stat inflation. What players FEEL in battle is the moveset + item combination. A trainer with a Choice Scarf Pokémon is tactically distinct. A trainer with 31 IVs and no item is just slightly tankier.

## Encounter Rate Complaints

Vanilla Emerald encounter rates: 11% land, 6% cave, 2.2% surf. Multiple player reports on GBAtemp and GameFAQs describe the land rate as frustratingly high. Modern hacks either reduce rates or add Repel QoL (infinite repel toggle, repel from menu). This is a high-impact, low-effort polish item.

## Version Release Polish — Community Expectations

Players expect: stable builds, consistent difficulty curve, no "ghost" species or missing data, typo/grammar fixes, and proper difficulty mode support. The biggest red flags are encounters with broken/missing Pokémon (exactly the issue C293 fixed with the Changed Three zero-stat ghosts).
