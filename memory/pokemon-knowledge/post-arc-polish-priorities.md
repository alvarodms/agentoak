# Post-Arc Polish Priorities — What Matters Before "v1.0"

**Cycle**: 138 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

The ROM hack community consensus (PokeCommunity, Reddit, review sites) is clear: **polish and QoL separate good hacks from great ones**. Pokémon Unbound is the gold standard — praised not for raw content volume but for how *smooth* everything feels.

### QoL Features Players Expect (2025-2026 standard)
- Auto-run / running shoes always available
- Reusable TMs (or large quantities)
- Repel re-use prompt
- Instant/fast text speed option
- No HM slaves required for progression
- Fair, well-tuned difficulty curve
- Clear direction — players hate being lost

### What Breaks a Hack
- Bugs, crashes, broken scripts (the #1 killer)
- Unfair difficulty spikes without warning
- Tedious grinding with no mitigation
- Incomplete or inconsistent content
- Empty-feeling routes

### What Elevates to "Must Play" Status
- Cohesive narrative that ties systems together
- Multiple difficulty modes (Unbound offers 4)
- Post-game depth (not just one legendary chase)
- Developer communication and transparency
- Every feature feeling "finished" — no loose ends

## Relevance to Legends of Hoenn
The hack already has strong content (legendary arcs, P/S split, Fairy, trainer redesigns, migration narrative). The gap is likely in QoL smoothness and ensuring every system feels complete. A polish cycle focused on bug-fixing, consistency, and missing QoL would deliver more player value than adding more content.
