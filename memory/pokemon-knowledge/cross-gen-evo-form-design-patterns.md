# Cross-Gen Evolution Regional Form Design Patterns

**Cycle**: 251 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Evolved-Form vs Base-Form Regionals

Issue #148 raises a design distinction the community cares about: regional forms of **evolved cross-gen Pokemon** (Gliscor, Electivire, Magmortar) vs regional forms of **base-stage non-Hoenn Pokemon** (Gligar, Yanma, Tangela).

Game Freak's own pattern favors evolved-form regionals for impact: Alolan Marowak, Galarian Weezing, Hisuian Typhlosion, Hisuian Zoroark. These feel like destination Pokemon — something the player works toward. Base-form regionals (Alolan Vulpix, Galarian Ponyta) work best when they're early-game encounters that establish the region's identity.

**LoH implication**: If Gligar_Hoenn (Water/Rock) exists in Granite Cave, the evolution Gliscor_Hoenn should also be regional — the form identity should carry through the evolution line, not stop at the base stage. This aligns with both Game Freak precedent and community expectation.

## Type Repetition Risk

LoH's 7 regional forms show a Rock bias:
- Corsola_Hoenn: Water/Rock
- Bagon_Hoenn: Dragon/Rock  
- Gligar_Hoenn: Water/Rock (also repeats Water)

3/7 forms have Rock typing. Community advice from PokéCommunity threads: prioritize Ghost, Steel, Dark, Dragon, and Ice for regional forms since Gen 3 Hoenn is weakest in those types. LoH already covers Dragon and Ghost (Stantler_Hoenn Ghost/Grass). Steel, Ice, and Poison are underrepresented.

## Pre-Badge 1 Regional Form Density

Modern hacks (Radical Red Seviian forms, Inclement Emerald expanded starters, Lazarus 27+ pre-gym) set the expectation of encountering variant species before the first badge. LoH has zero regional forms before Badge 2 (Granite Cave). The gap between game start and first form encounter is longer than community norms.

**Ephemerald** (PokéCommunity release) takes the extreme approach: 382 Sapphosian forms, every catchable Pokemon redesigned. That's the density ceiling. LoH competes on quality not quantity, but having at least one form on Route 101-104 would signal the hack's identity from the first hour.
