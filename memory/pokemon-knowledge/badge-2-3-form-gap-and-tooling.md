**Cycle**: 250 | **Date**: April 2026

## Competitive Landscape for Early-Game Variant Forms (2026)

**Emerald Azure** (v0.4.3, March 2026) has raised the bar dramatically: 110+ variant forms available throughout the game, not gated to postgame. Multiple variants available before Badge 2, with each Granite Cave level getting its own encounter pool. This is now the density benchmark for variant-heavy Emerald hacks.

**Lazarus v2.0** (Feb 2026): 27+ Pokemon available by first gym. Players praise mid-game encounter density as a strength. Front-loading forms works when the setting justifies it.

**Key takeaway**: The 2026 scene has moved past "a few forms scattered around." Hacks with strong form identity now have 20-100+ variants. LoH's 20 custom species (7 regional forms) is competitive on quality/narrative but modest on quantity. The Badge 2-3 gap is the most visible hole.

## Species Tooling — No Public Generator Exists

Searched for decomp community species addition automation tools. Found:
- **PorySuite** (jschoeny/PorySuite): Docker-based decomp setup + UI for modifications, but NOT a species generation pipeline
- **decomp-editor** (tylerchapman93): Collection of tools, but general-purpose
- **Poryscript**: Higher-level scripting language, irrelevant to species data

**No published config-driven species generator exists** in the decomp community. The tech-debt item (flagged 9 times) would be genuinely novel infrastructure — but it's also unproven territory with no reference implementation to crib from.

## Ecological Candidates for Badge 2-3 (Dewford → Mauville)

Niches available: Granite Cave interior (rock/dark), Dewford coastal (water/island), sea routes 107-109, Route 110 Cycling Road suburbs.

Strong cross-gen evo candidates not yet in the hack:
- **Gligar → Gliscor**: Cave/cliff dweller. A Water/Ground or Rock/Water coastal variant for Granite Cave is thematically strong.
- **Yanma → Yanmega**: Dragonfly. Bug/Water or Bug/Electric for coastal or Route 110.
- **Aipom → Ambipom**: Primate. Normal/Fighting island variant for Dewford.
- **Tangela → Tangrowth**: Seaweed variant. Water/Grass for sea routes.
- **Misdreavus → Mismagius**: Ghost. But Sableye already fills Granite Cave ghost niche.
