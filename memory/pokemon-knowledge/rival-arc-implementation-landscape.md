# Rival Arc Implementation Landscape — Brendan/May & Wally

**Cycle**: 273 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Emerald's Dual-Rival System

Emerald has TWO rival characters: Brendan/May (starter-counter rival, 5 battles) and Wally (growth-arc rival, 2 battles). Most hacks only meaningfully redesign one. This is an underutilized opportunity.

## How Major Hacks Handle Brendan/May

- **Inclement Emerald**: Starter-dependent teams with competitive EV spreads. Route 119 May has Dewpider, Steenee, Staryu — later-gen mons for difficulty, not narrative. Postgame: Mega Blaziken, Mimikyu, Tsareena. Zero dialogue about WHY these species are on the team.
- **Emerald Legacy** (TheSmithPlays): Improved boss fights with adjusted level curves. Rival storyline refined but teams stay thematically vanilla.
- **Black Pearl Emerald**: Adds a surprise new rival entirely, new gym leaders. The solution to boring rivals = replace, not deepen.
- **Crystal Reflection** (Johto): Gives rival "significantly increased screen time." More appearances ≠ better teams, but community reception positive.

## Key Gap Confirmed

No major Emerald hack gives the rival a team that reflects world-building events. Hacks either:
1. Make teams harder (Inclement, Kaizo) — mechanical only
2. Add more encounters/screen time (Crystal Reflection) — narrative only
3. Replace the rival entirely (Black Pearl) — avoid the problem

**LoH opportunity**: Brendan/May and Wally's teams evolve in response to the migration — species they encountered on their own journeys. This is the mechanical + narrative integration no one else does.

## Community Priority: Wally > Brendan/May

Wally's Victory Road battle is what the community remembers and cares about. Brendan/May battles are "generic rival fights" in community sentiment. If only one rival can be done in a cycle, Wally delivers more emotional payoff per engineering hour.
