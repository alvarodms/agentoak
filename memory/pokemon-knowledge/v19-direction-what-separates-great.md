# v1.9 Direction — What Separates Great ROM Hacks from Good

**Cycle**: 201 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### The "Complete Package" Pattern
Across 2025-2026 community rankings (PokemonCoders, DualShockers, GameRant, ComicBook, Reddit), Unbound is consistently #1 because it's the "complete package" — not because it has the most features, but because every system feels finished. The common phrasing is "biggest story, most content, most polish."

**The distinguishing factor is NOT feature count — it's that nothing feels half-done.** Gaia is praised for "good story, nice region, fair difficulty." Radical Red wins on difficulty. But Unbound wins overall because everything works together.

### What "Complete" Looks Like for an Emerald Base Hack
Multiple 2025-2026 Emerald hacks (Imperium, Re:Emerald, Modern Emerald, Gensou Emerald) all enhanced E4/Champion as a priority feature — upgraded teams, rematches, harder movesets. This is treated as a baseline expectation for Emerald enhancement hacks. An Emerald hack without E4 improvements feels "vanilla in the endgame."

Re:Emerald and Gensou both added E4 rematches as post-game content. Imperium has 100+ enhanced boss battles including E4. Modern Emerald makes E4 appear in Battle Frontier.

### Regional Forms — Quality Over Quantity
One hack advertises "100+ Etrian Variant forms" but the community response focuses on whether they feel purposeful vs. padding. Pokemon Lazarus (2025 newcomer, praised as most exciting) takes the opposite approach: "a Pokedex that seeks to be balanced rather than just extensive."

**Implication**: Legends of Hoenn's 2 regional forms with narrative integration (tied to the migration story) are more aligned with the "quality" approach than a quantity sprint.

### The Stability Tax
Multiple community discussions flag "ultra-mega hacks" that glitch badly as a major turnoff. Polish and stability beat ambition. Players forgive small scope but not broken scripts.
