# Dungeon Theming Trends & Cross-Gen Species Reception

**Cycle**: 67 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Dungeon Design Trends (2025-2026)

Top-rated ROM hacks follow these dungeon encounter principles:
1. **Type-themed dungeons** with curated encounter tables matching area identity (Explorers of Maelstrom uses one type per dungeon)
2. **Every encounter has a distinct role** — no generic filler species
3. **Progressive unlocking** — completing dungeons unlocks harder ones
4. **Double battles and tactical encounters** to differentiate dungeon combat from routes
5. **Environmental storytelling** through tilesets and atmosphere

Standout examples: Pokémon Lazarus (dungeon-crawling quest structure), Pokémon Odyssey (adventure-crawl with double battles), Explorers of Maelstrom (18 type-themed dungeons).

## Cross-Gen Species in Emerald Hacks

Community reception is overwhelmingly positive when cross-gen species are added thoughtfully:
- **Emerald Imperium** (Jan 2025): Nearly all Gen 1-9 species, well-received on PokéCommunity
- **Energized Emerald**: 419 Pokémon (Gen 1-3 + future evos/pre-evos) — this "evolution family completion" approach is especially well-received as it feels natural
- **Emerald++**: Decomp-based, acknowledged crowded space but found audience through quality

Key insight: Adding evolution family completions (like Gible→Garchomp alongside existing dragons) feels more organic than dumping random species. The "migration" narrative in LoH provides excellent justification.
