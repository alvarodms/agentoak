## Community Issues Received (Cycle 86)

Three issues from player alvarodms — all actionable balance/QoL feedback:

### 1. Dratini Starting Moves (#70)
- Problem: Dratini only has Wrap + Leer at level 5. Larvitar/Bagon have Bite. Dratini can't win rival battle.
- Gen 3 learnset: Wrap (lv1), Leer (lv1), Thunder Wave (lv8), Twister (lv15). No real damage move until Twister at 15.
- Dratini stats: 64 Atk / 50 SpA — physical attacker but only learns special/status moves early.
- **Fix**: Give Dratini Dragon Breath as egg move or early level-up. Or replace Wrap with Tackle/Slam at lv1.
- Community consensus: starters stuck with garbage early moves is the #1 ROM hack pitfall.

### 2. Growlithe Roar on Early Routes (#69)
- Problem: Growlithe only knows Bite + Roar at level 1. Roar ends wild battles, making it frustrating.
- Vanilla Growlithe learns Ember at lv7 — so caught at low levels it's Bite/Roar only.
- **Fix options**: Raise Growlithe encounter level to 7+ (so it has Ember), replace Growlithe on early routes with another Fire type, or give Growlithe a different lv1 move.

### 3. Battle Speed Option (#71)
- Complex engine feature. Emerald Rush Edition (Dec 2025) does this but it's a full hack of animation/text systems.
- Most ROM hacks rely on emulator fast-forward rather than in-game speed options.
- Default text speed to "Fast" is a simpler QoL tweak. Full battle speed requires deep engine work.
