# Regional Form Delivery & Ability Design Patterns

**Cycle**: 195 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### Radical Red's Seviian Forms — Postgame Gatekeeping
Radical Red's custom regional forms (Seviian Forms) are restricted: trainers in the main game don't use them, and only one is available pre-postgame. This prevents custom forms from feeling jarring to players who prefer a vanilla-adjacent experience. Validates Legends of Hoenn's approach of delivering regional forms as postgame quest rewards.

### Weak Armor on Corsola — Community Consensus Is Negative
Smogon and competitive analyses of Galarian Corsola unanimously recommend Cursed Body over Weak Armor. Weak Armor lowers Defense when hit (counterproductive for a defensive Ghost) in exchange for Speed boosts that a slow tank can't use. The Hoennian Corsola (Ghost/Rock, ~410 BST) needs to decide: is it a bulky utility mon or a fragile setup sweeper? If bulky, Weak Armor is the wrong ability. If offensive/setup, the ~410 BST may not support it. Consider Rock Head (thematic for fossilized coral, synergizes with Rock-type recoil moves like Head Smash) or a custom ability choice.

### Ghost/Rock Type Niche
Ghost/Rock is extremely rare — only Runerigus (Gen 8) shares it officially. It has useful immunities (Normal, Fighting) and resistances (Poison, Bug, Flying) but is weak to Ground, Water, Grass, Ghost, Dark, Steel — six weaknesses. This makes it fragile unless BST is allocated heavily to defenses. At ~410 BST, stat allocation is the most critical design decision.

### Quest Reward Static Encounters
Pokémon Axiom Version and Emerald Horizons both use quest completion to gate special species. The community-validated pattern: scripted givemon/static encounter at a meaningful location (not just "NPC hands you a Pokéball"). Emerald Essence does Jirachi as a Dex-completion reward NPC gift — functional but less memorable.
