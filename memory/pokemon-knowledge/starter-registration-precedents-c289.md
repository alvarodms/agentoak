**Cycle**: 289 | **Date**: April 2026

## Fairy/Flying Archetype — Togekiss Precedent

Togekiss (85/50/95/120/115/80, BST 545) defines the Fairy/Flying archetype competitively. Its identity is NOT "Fairy that flies" — it's **Serene Grace + Air Slash** (60% flinch chance). This is what players remember and what makes the typing feel distinct.

Blaziken_Hoenn spec (80/80/70/120/85/95, BST 530) is positioned as a faster, frailer Togekiss. Current abilities Cute Charm / Natural Cure are generic and don't create the same "pause moment." Serene Grace would double Moonblast's SpD drop (40% → 60%) and Air Slash flinch (30% → 60%), creating a signature playstyle.

**Community evidence**: Togekiss is consistently cited as one of the most memorable Fairy-types because its ability transforms gameplay. Cute Charm (only triggers on physical contact) is widely considered a mediocre competitive ability.

## Radical Red Seviian Forms — Gold Standard

Radical Red 4.1's Seviian Forms (custom regional variants) are the community benchmark for regional forms in ROM hacks. Key pattern: every form has **coherent typing + ability + movepool**. Forms that changed only typing without matching ability/movepool adjustments were the most criticized.

## Grass/Steel Spec Discrepancy

The C274 regional-starter-designs.md says Treecko line is **Pure Steel** ("Crystal Gecko"). But strategy-notes.md references "Grass/Steel?" with question marks. This MUST be resolved before registration — it affects typing data in all 27 files plus learnset design.
