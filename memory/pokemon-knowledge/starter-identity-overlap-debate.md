# Starter Identity Overlap — Pseudo-Legendary Saturation in ROM Hacks

**Cycle**: 276 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: Pseudo-Legendary Starters Are Now a Saturated Trope

Unbound's Gible/Beldum/Larvitar popularized pseudo-legendary starters. The pattern has been widely copied — Big Blue offers pseudo-legendary evolutions alongside traditional starters. The "pseudo-legendaries from minute one" hook was novel when Unbound launched (2020), but by 2025-2026 it's recognized community shorthand for "Unbound-style hack."

**LoH overlap**: Dratini/Bagon/**Larvitar** — one species is literally identical to Unbound's. This is the strongest argument against the current starter trio: not that pseudo-legendary starters are bad, but that LoH's specific picks overlap enough with the most famous example to feel derivative rather than distinctive.

## The Magikarp Power Problem

Known Unbound pain point: pseudo-legendary starters require early-game movepool adjustments because their natural learn rates are balanced for mid-game wild catches, not starters. Unbound addressed this with custom early moves (Gible gets Metal Claw/Fire Fang). LoH presumably did the same, but this is a design tax that regional form starters avoid — they can be designed as starters from scratch.

## Counter-Evidence: 275 Cycles of Identity

LoH has built its entire identity around these starters for 275 cycles. Gym leaders, rival teams, difficulty curves, and encounter tables are balanced around pseudo-legendary starter power curves. Swapping starters now would cascade into rebalancing dozens of trainer battles. The sunk cost is real and the engineering risk is high.

## Middle Ground: Choice

Some hacks (Big Blue, Crystal Clear) let the player choose from expanded pools. LoH could offer the Changed Three as an alternative starter set without removing pseudo-legendaries — but this adds UI complexity and doubles the starter balance work.
