# Release Timing & Quest Chain Patterns

**Cycle**: 234 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Ship-Then-Iterate is the Dominant Model

Pokémon Lazarus launched v1.0 in Nov 2025 with full region + story, then dropped 2.0 in early 2026 with expanded content. This is the standard pattern — ship a complete milestone, gather feedback, iterate. Hacks that delay shipping to add "one more thing" often lose community momentum.

Transparent development practices are trending: DontJoelMe (Lazarus dev) provides itemized progress updates via Discord, and the community responds positively to visible milestones being hit.

## Multi-Stage Quest Chains Are Community Expectation

Pokémon Unbound has 84 missions total, 27 postgame-only. Quests use progressive discovery — find NPC, accept, fulfill conditions. Multi-stage legendary quests are the norm, not the exception. "Every legendary that you encounter has a story event tied to it" is praised as a design principle.

No community evidence supports COMBINING multi-part quests into single encounters. The trend runs the opposite direction — more stages, more narrative justification, more progressive revelation. Unbound's mission HQ even tracks quest progress visually.

## Implication for v2.1 Planning

The evidence supports:
1. **Shipping v2.1 as-is** rather than extending — the three pillars are complete, and delaying for hypothetical additions loses momentum
2. **Keeping Deoxys quests separate** — multi-stage quest chains are what top hacks do and what players praise
3. **Deferring custom species work to v2.2** — Lazarus model shows major additions belong in a follow-up version
