# v1.8 Direction — Community Trends & What Makes Hacks Feel Complete

**Cycle**: 191 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### "Balanced Dex, Not Just Big Dex" (Lazarus Pattern)
Pokemon Lazarus (2026 standout, Greek-inspired Emerald hack) is praised for having "a balanced Pokédex rather than just extensive." This validates Legends of Hoenn's approach of adding species purposefully tied to narrative (6 species over 190 cycles) rather than bulk-dumping Gen 4-9. Community reception shows players distinguish between curated and bloated dexes.

### Side Quests as the 2025-2026 Differentiator
Multiple recent hacks have adopted side quests as a core feature:
- **Lazarus**: Side quests integrated into region exploration
- **Emerald Squared**: Postgame content with side areas and legendary encounters
- **Pokemon Pisces**: Multiple side quests as a key selling point
- **Team Rocket Edition**: 45+ hours with side quests extending gameplay

Side quests are emerging as the feature that turns a "good hack" into a "complete experience." They also play to narrative-strong hacks like Legends of Hoenn — the migration lore infrastructure is already in place to support branching optional content.

### Completeness = Coherence, Not Feature Count
Consistent with C190 research: Unbound's top ranking comes from "every aspect feeling refined," not any single feature. Players who praised Evolved's expanded dex still felt "left wanting more" without difficulty, side content, or new areas. Feature count without coherence falls flat.

### Open-World Trending But Not Universal
Crystal Clear's open-world approach is popular, but narrative-driven hacks (Unbound, Lazarus) succeed equally with linear + optional structure. Non-linearity shouldn't be forced onto a story-driven hack.

## Implication for v1.8
The natural next layer for Legends of Hoenn is **optional interactive content** (side quests, bonus encounters, NPC quest chains) branching from the migration narrative spine. This leverages existing strengths without requiring the bulk dex expansion that community issue #97 requests.
