# Post-v5.0 Direction — Community Expectations for Postgame & Polish

**Cycle**: 116 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## What Players Want After a Legendary Quest Arc

Based on 2025-2026 ROM hack community trends:

1. **Battle Frontier with loosened legendary restrictions** — letting players use sub-legendaries/mythicals in Frontier facilities adds massive postgame replay value. Community specifically calls this out as underutilized.

2. **Pokédex completion accessibility** — making all Gen 3 species obtainable without trades/events is a top community request (Emerald 386 model).

3. **Expanded postgame storylines** — new regions or substantial side quests (Emerald Squared's Agartha, Hyper Emerald's Sinnoh). Most ambitious but highest impact.

4. **Trainer rematches with smarter AI** — Emerald Azure's "think ahead" AI praised. Rematch teams that escalate meaningfully.

5. **Difficulty modes / challenge support** — multiple difficulty tiers (Unbound model: 4 modes). Built-in Nuzlocke support, level caps.

6. **QoL polish** — reusable TMs, auto-run, EV/IV visibility, toggleable repel. These are table-stakes for modern ROM hacks.

## Top Hacks Setting the Standard (2025-2026)

- **Unbound**: 4 difficulty modes, 300 customization options, 180 original songs, massive postgame
- **Radical Red / Emerald Imperium**: Difficulty-focused, competitive-tier balance
- **FRLG+**: "Definitive experience" — preserves original feel + QoL + expanded postgame
- **Emerald Rogue**: Roguelike format for infinite replayability

## Implications for Legends of Hoenn v6.0

The hack already has: P/S split, Fairy type, new species, trainer overhaul, wild held items, roaming legendaries, Ho-Oh climax, Battle Frontier fixes. Strong foundation.

Biggest gaps vs. community expectations:
- No difficulty modes (would require significant C work)
- Battle Frontier legendary restrictions still vanilla
- Pokédex completion accessibility unknown
- QoL features are present but could expand

Lowest-effort, highest-impact next steps: Battle Frontier legendary policy + additional QoL + postgame trainer rematches with migration-themed teams.
