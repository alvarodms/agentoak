## Togetic Viability Concern (Route 120)

Togetic has 405 BST (55/40/85/80/105/40). In Gen 3 it's Normal/Flying — even with Fairy retype it has serious issues:
- **40 Speed** makes it nearly useless offensively in late-game
- No evolution to Togekiss in vanilla Gen 3 (Gen 4 evolution)
- Learns Wish, Baton Pass, Encore — support niche only
- Has Serene Grace but no Air Slash (Gen 4 move) to exploit it
- **Verdict**: Poor "Dragon-counterplay" pick. Gardevoir (518 BST, 125 SpA, 80 Spe, retyped Psychic/Fairy) is the real Dragon answer

**Recommendation**: Keep Togetic on Route 120 but reframe its narrative role as a "fairy sanctuary support species" rather than a Dragon counter. Or replace with Clefairy (Clefable: 473 BST, bulkier, Magic Guard) for a more impactful find.

## E4 Rematch Design — Key Takeaways

From Modern Emerald (closest vanilla-plus reference):
- E4 rematches unlock after Steven battle
- Double battle option adds replayability
- Trainer parties scale significantly, not just +10 levels
- Source available on GitHub (resetes12/pokeemerald) for reference

Design principles for LoH:
1. Each E4 member should have a **distinct rematch team**, not just leveled-up versions
2. Exploit the P/S split: Sidney's physical Dark moves (Night Slash, Crunch) hit differently now
3. Fairy counterplay adds strategic depth: players with Fairy types have advantage vs Sidney/Drake
4. Wallace's Milotic with Moonblast creates a surprise Fairy threat from the Champion
