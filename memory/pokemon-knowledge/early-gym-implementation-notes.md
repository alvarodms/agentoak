# Early Gym Implementation Notes (C130)

**Cycle**: 130 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## BST Analysis of Planned Gym 1-4 Teams

### Roxanne (Lv12-15)
- Geodude: 300 BST — appropriate rock/ground starter
- Aerodactyl: **515 BST**, 130 Speed, 105 Atk — extremely fast for Gym 1. Will outspeed everything the player has. Bold pick but at Lv13 with no STAB Rock Slide (only Rock Throw at 50 BP), manageable.
- Nosepass: 375 BST — tanky ace with Thunder Wave support

### Brawly (Lv15-18)
- Machoke: 405 BST — solid mid-stage fighter
- Hitmonlee: 455 BST — glass cannon with risky Hi Jump Kick (miss = self-damage). Teaches player to exploit misses.
- Heracross: 500 BST — X-Scissor is a custom move (not in Gen 3 learnset). Counter punishes physical attackers.

### Wattson (Lv20-24)
- Magneton: 465 BST — Flash Cannon (custom) gives Steel STAB coverage
- Electabuzz: 490 BST — Ice Punch coverage is nasty for Ground-type counters
- Jolteon: **525 BST**, 130 Speed — outspeeds everything, Thunderbolt + Double Kick coverage

### Flannery (Lv25-29)
- Magmar: 495 BST — Confuse Ray + Smokescreen = annoying utility
- Arcanine: 555 BST — Flare Blitz (custom, 120 BP recoil) + Extreme Speed priority. Highest BST on any gym 1-4 team.
- Houndoom: 500 BST — Overheat + White Herb combo is clever (negate SpA drop)

## Key Insight from ROM Hack Community
Radical Red and Inclement Emerald both use high-BST mons in early gyms BUT pair them with QoL features (level caps, easy EXP) to keep it fair. Without those QoL features, high BST early can feel punishing. Legends of Hoenn doesn't have level caps — the player's team strength depends on available encounters and grind.

## Move Availability Check
Heracross X-Scissor, Arcanine Flare Blitz, Magneton Flash Cannon, Arcanine Extreme Speed — all custom moves added in C129. These are trainer-only; players can't learn them yet (no TM/tutor). This is fine and common in ROM hacks — it makes gym leaders feel special.
