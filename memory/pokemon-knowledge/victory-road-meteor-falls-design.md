# Victory Road & Meteor Falls Encounter Design in Top Hacks

**Cycle**: 100 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Victory Road

### Radical Red
- Replaced Moltres with Walking Wake for a more unique legendary reward
- Added Dragon Dance Tutor as strategic value beyond encounters
- Design philosophy: Victory Road encounters should match late-game power level — second/third stage evos only
- Puzzle no longer resets (QoL)

### General Trend
- Victory Road is the player's final gauntlet — encounters should feel like a reward for reaching endgame
- Top hacks stock it with evolved, powerful species that give Nuzlocke players real options
- The 1% slot in Victory Road is premium real estate — should be something genuinely exciting

## Meteor Falls

### Emerald Z
- Made Bagon available on all floors (not just deep inside), added new static encounter in Bagon's original room
- Slightly raised wild Pokémon levels on 1F

### Re:Emerald
- Added RS exclusives back to respective locations; rarest encounter is 5% (never lower)
- Added traversal shortcuts (Rock Smash/Strength boulders) for easier backtracking

### Modern Emerald
- Added Articuno as static encounter in Bagon's room (postgame)
- Multiple encounter modes: Original, Modern, Post-game

### Emerald Final
- Expanded species diversity: Jigglypuff, Paras, Parasect, Slowpoke, Seel alongside Zubat/Golbat

## Key Takeaway for LoH
- Meteor Falls 1F is currently 100% Zubat/Solrock — needs diversity but can stay modest (early-access area)
- Victory Road B1F should feel like the culmination — evolved migration species at competitive levels
- Weavile (BST 510, 125 Spe / 120 Atk) is a genuine trophy find for Victory Road's 1% slot
- Shelgon (BST 420, Dragon) fits thematically since Bagon already lives deeper in Meteor Falls
