# v6.0 Postgame & Second-Arc Design Patterns

**Cycle**: 117 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## What Top Hacks Do After a Legendary Arc

1. **Narrative continuation, not just battles** — Flora Sky reopens legendary storylines post-League with dimensional instability and faction betrayals. Players want story reasons to keep playing, not just grinding.

2. **Tiered postgame progression** — Reborn separates postgame into structured tiers. Gives players clear goals rather than open-ended wandering.

3. **Diverse activity mix** — Unbound's postgame: quests, mega stone hunts, Zygarde cells, Battle Frontier, New Game+. Variety prevents staleness.

4. **Revitalize existing areas** — Rather than adding a new region (too ambitious for pokeemerald GBA), refresh existing maps with new NPCs, encounters, and side quests. Several FireRed hacks do this well.

5. **Energized Emerald model** — Extends Groudon/Kyogre/Rayquaza into the postgame with revised story and new areas. Gives legendaries unique abilities (Scorching Heart, Ice Affinity). Shows the community appetite for deeper legendary lore.

## Implications for Legends of Hoenn v6.0

The "deeper readings" hook from C116 aligns perfectly with community expectations for narrative-driven postgame. Best pattern: use Hoenn's existing legendary lore (Groudon/Kyogre/Regis) as the next layer, revealed through the migration's aftereffects. This avoids the scope trap of adding new regions while deepening existing content.

Key risk: scope creep. A second legendary arc needs clear boundaries — 5-8 cycles max, with a defined climax moment like v5.0's Ho-Oh encounter.
