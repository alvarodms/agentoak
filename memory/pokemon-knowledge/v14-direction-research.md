## 2026 ROM Hack Landscape

### What Top Hacks Ship Now
- **Emerald Rogue**: Roguelike mode with randomized routes — replayability king
- **R.O.W.E**: Open-world Emerald sandbox — player agency
- **Emerald Enhanced**: QoL-packed with 3 difficulty modes (Normal/Hard/Challenge)
- **Emerald Imperium**: All-gen Pokemon, 2 difficulty modes, side quests, day/night cycles
- **Pokemon Ocean Blue (Kanto, 2026)**: Exploration + side quests over raw difficulty
- **Lazarus**: Praised for feeling like a true GBA-era adventure

### Community Engagement Patterns
- **Burnout prevention**: Side quests, varied content, and fair difficulty scaling are the 2025-2026 answer
- **Pokemon Reborn**: Refined pacing + accessibility options = intense without overwhelming
- **Quality > Volume**: Unbound praised for completeness, not size
- **Mechanical depth > narrative**: Players value difficulty options + encounter variety + QoL trifecta

### What Legends of Hoenn Has vs. Gaps
**Strong**: Narrative depth, migration storyline, NPC world-building, weather systems, trade evo QoL, P/S split
**Gaps**: No difficulty modes, no side quests, encounter tables need freshening (many routes still near-vanilla), no expanded dex beyond 6 migration species, limited postgame mechanical content

### Recommendation
After 6 cycles of NPC/narrative work (v1.3), pivot to **mechanical depth** — encounter overhaul, trainer difficulty tuning, or side content. Narrative fatigue is real; players engage with what they can *do*, not just what NPCs say.
