# Scripted Route Events & Overworld Encounters in ROM Hacks

**Cycle**: 149 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## What Makes Scripted Events Memorable

1. **Unbound's mission system**: Side quests with overworld encounters, puzzles, and NPC triggers. Players cite the mission log as a key differentiator. Legendary quests are multi-step with environmental storytelling.

2. **Weather as mechanic, not flavor**: Inclement Emerald and Radical Red both use route weather to shape trainer teams (Swift Swim, Thunder in rain). But neither adds *narrative* scripted events tied to weather — this is an open design space.

3. **Player agency**: The best scripted events give the player a choice or reward exploration. A forced cutscene is less memorable than stumbling upon something unexpected.

## Design Principles for Route 119 Migration Event

- **Trigger**: Rain-dependent or flag-gated (post-Weather Institute). Player returns and something new happens.
- **Payoff**: A rare migration species appears, or Dr. Hartley has new dialogue connecting weather readings to the migration.
- **Scale**: Keep it small — one scripted encounter or NPC scene. Don't try to build a full quest system.
- **Precedent**: The existing postgame legendary arc already uses flag-gated overworld events successfully. This would be the first *mid-game* scripted event.
