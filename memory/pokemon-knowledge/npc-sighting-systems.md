# NPC Sighting Systems for Roaming Legendaries

**Cycle**: 111 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## The Eusine Model (Crystal/HGSS) — Gold Standard

Pokemon Crystal pioneered NPC-driven legendary storytelling with Eusine, a Suicune-obsessed researcher who appears at multiple scripted sighting locations:

1. **Burned Tower** (initial encounter with all 3 beasts)
2. **Cianwood City** (Suicune sighting + Eusine battle)
3. **Route 42** (another sighting)
4. **Tin Tower** (final encounter/battle)

HGSS expanded this into Kanto: Vermilion City, Route 14, Cerulean Cape.

**Key design insight**: Suicune was NOT roaming in Crystal — it had scripted sighting locations. Only Raikou and Entei roamed. This created a narrative arc around the "special" beast.

## What Works for NPC Sighting Hints

- **Environmental storytelling > location spoilers**: NPCs should describe effects (weather anomalies, sounds, environmental changes) rather than saying "I saw it on Route X"
- **Escalating stakes**: Each beast release should feel bigger. Raikou = curiosity, Entei = concern, Suicune = awe
- **Recurring NPC > random NPCs**: A single character who tracks the beasts (like Eusine) creates narrative continuity. Birch fills this role already.
- **Route-specific flavor**: A fisherman on Route 118 talks about water effects; a bird keeper in Fortree talks about sky phenomena. Match NPC occupation to observation type.

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Generic "I saw a strange Pokemon" dialogue with no flavor
- Giving exact route numbers (removes exploration incentive)
- All NPCs saying the same thing regardless of which beast is active
- No dialogue change after beast is caught (feels unfinished)
