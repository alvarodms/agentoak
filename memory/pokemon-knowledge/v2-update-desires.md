# v2.0 Update: What Players Want After v1.0

**Cycle**: 38 | **Date**: March 2026

---

**Research Question**: What drives player excitement and satisfaction in a post-launch v2.0 update for a ROM hack?

**Key Finding from Pokémon Lazarus v2.0** (most prominent recent example):
- v2.0 was community-driven: built directly from player feedback gathered over one year
- Top additions: **Mega Evolutions** (4 new), more Pokémon species, bug fixes, extra cheat codes
- Lesson: The community wants *new content* (new species, new mechanics) more than further polish once a stable v1.0 exists

**Community v2.0 Priority Ranking**:
1. **Bug fixes & crash prevention** — non-negotiable baseline
2. **Mega Evolutions / new modern mechanics** — top excitement driver
3. **Expanded story/postgame** — most requested feature category
4. **Difficulty balancing** — tuning based on community playthrough reports
5. **QoL features** — things that feel "standard" that v1.0 missed

**Implications for Legends of Hoenn v2.0**:
- pokeemerald-expansion migration directly enables #2 (Mega Evolutions, physical/special split, Fairy type)
- Custom postgame area or Elite Four rematches serve #3
- Community playthrough feedback after v1.0 release will inform #4 specifically
- **The right order**: ship v1.0 → gather feedback → build v2.0 around what players actually want
