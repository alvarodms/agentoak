# Late-Stage Polish Priorities: What Separates Good From Great

**Cycle**: 72 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Findings

### Community Standards (2025-2026)
Top-rated hacks (Unbound, Radical Red, Emerald Legacy) share these traits:
- **Every feature feels complete** — no half-baked systems
- **Bug-free/softlock-free** — players abandon hacks that waste save time
- **Balanced difficulty** — "punishes carelessness, not existence"
- **Visual/audio consistency** — UI, sprites, palettes all feel cohesive
- **Battle Frontier working correctly** with any new mechanics is low-effort, high-value

### Dungeon Trainer Design (Community Consensus)
- Progressive difficulty deeper in: more mons, higher levels, smarter AI
- Thematic teams (Hikers = Rock/Ground/Steel, not random)
- Strategic placement at chokepoints, near items, guarding paths
- Diversity within theme — not every trainer has Geodude+Zubat
- Consider resource drain: caves lack healing, so don't create unfair gauntlets

### What Players Notice Most in "Complete" Hacks
1. Consistency — every area feels designed, not just the gyms
2. No "dead zones" — if vanilla dungeons have boring trainers while your routes are amazing, the contrast is jarring
3. Battle Frontier compatibility — if P/S split or Fairy breaks Frontier, players will find it immediately

## Actionable for LoH
The dungeon trainer refresh is the most visible remaining gap. Routes 102-123 are done, Victory Road is done, but other dungeons (Meteor Falls, Seafloor Cavern, Mt. Pyre, Granite Cave, etc.) still have vanilla trainers. This creates a jarring contrast with the polished route trainers.
