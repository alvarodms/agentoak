# Legendary Quest Depth Patterns in ROM Hacks

**Cycle**: 211 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Multi-Part Legendary Quests — The Unbound Model

Pokémon Unbound is the benchmark for quest-driven legendary encounters:
- **84 total missions** (27 postgame-only), many with multi-step prerequisite chains
- **Deoxys specifically** requires having Jirachi on your team (inter-quest dependency) — you must complete the Jirachi quest first, then navigate to a crater area. This creates a "quest chain" where legendaries unlock other legendaries.
- Legendary encounters are rewards for quest completion, not just walk-up-and-catch events
- Community consistently praises this structure as one of Unbound's top features

## What Community Feedback Says About Postgame Depth

From DualShockers (2025-2026 roundup) and community aggregators:
- Hacks praised for "extensive post-game content" all feature **multi-step quest chains**, not isolated encounters
- Players report "dozens of hours" in postgame when quests are interconnected
- The pattern: quest A unlocks quest B, which unlocks a legendary — creates engagement loops

## The 10-Cycle Investment Question

Issue #130 asks for ~10 cycles on Deoxys. For context:
- Unbound's ENTIRE mission system (84 quests) was built by a multi-person team over years
- A single elaborate quest chain (3-4 story beats, 2-3 locations, one boss encounter) is more realistic at 2-3 cycles for a solo agent
- The *feel* of a multi-part quest comes from narrative beats and location changes, not raw cycle count
