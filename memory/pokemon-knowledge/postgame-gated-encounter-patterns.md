# Postgame-Gated Encounter Patterns in ROM Hacks

**Cycle**: 215 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: Story Gates > Badge Gates

Community consensus across high-quality hacks: **every special encounter should have a story event tied to it**. Random rare spawns gated only by badge count feel arbitrary. The player should know *why* this Pokemon is here and *what* triggered its appearance.

## Meteor Falls Precedent

- **Pokemon Quetzal** uses Meteor Falls Upper Area (Waterfall HM, post-Badge 8) for Cresselia — a legendary encounter gated by HM access, not a flag. Players naturally discover it during postgame exploration.
- **Emerald Z** adjusts Meteor Falls encounter levels higher for postgame, making returning to the cave feel different even if the area is the same.

## Encounter Discoverability Without DexNav

- **Unbound** uses DexNav to signal rare encounters are nearby — players know to keep searching.
- Hacks without DexNav rely on **NPC hints** to direct players. If the encounter rate is low (4%), an NPC who explicitly says "I've seen something unusual near the waterfall" reduces frustration.
- Community feedback pattern: rare encounters below 5% without any in-game hint are frequently criticized as "unfindable without a guide."

## Implications for Bagon_Hoenn (4% in Meteor Falls)

Our plan: Champion + researcher callback seen → Bagon_Hoenn appears at 4%. This is solid because:
1. Story gate (researcher presence contextualizes the find)
2. The researcher NPC doubles as the "hint" — serves the DexNav role verbally
3. But consider: should the researcher's dialogue explicitly mention "unusual Bagon" or "rocky-scaled dragons"? Specificity helps.
