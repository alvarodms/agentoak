# Mid-Game Polish: What Separates Good from Great

**Cycle**: 95 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Community Consensus (2025-2026)

Great ROM hacks are not defined by feature count but by **every feature feeling finished and purposeful**. Mid-game is the most common weak spot — even top hacks like Unbound have pacing dips there.

## The 6 Polish Dimensions

1. **Pacing** — No dead zones where nothing happens. Every route segment should have encounters, trainers, or narrative that maintain momentum.
2. **Difficulty curve smoothness** — No sudden spikes or troughs. Badge 4-6 range is where most hacks lose players.
3. **Route density** — Empty routes are immediately noticed. "Some routes feel empty compared to denser 2025 hacks" is a common complaint.
4. **Bug-free experience** — Crashes and broken scripts kill hacks faster than bad design. Polish pass = bug hunting.
5. **Purposeful NPCs** — Every NPC should do something: world-build, hint, or create atmosphere. Filler NPCs are obvious.
6. **QoL consistency** — If you have reusable TMs, players expect other QoL too. Inconsistency feels unfinished.

## For LoH Cycle 95 Specifically

The Weather Institute NPC is the last narrative beat before the postgame quest chain. This is a **foreshadowing pivot point** — it should create anticipation, not just deliver exposition. No existing ROM hack has notably used the Weather Institute for narrative foreshadowing, making this an opportunity for LoH to be distinctive.

"Mid-game polish" beyond the NPC could mean: verifying trainer level curves, checking route encounter variety, ensuring no dead zones between Lavaridge and Fortree.
