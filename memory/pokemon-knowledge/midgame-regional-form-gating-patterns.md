# Mid-Game Regional Form Gating Patterns

**Cycle**: 231 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Radical Red's Postgame-Gating Approach

Radical Red (v4.1) gates nearly all custom regional forms ("Seviian Forms") to the postgame. Only one is available before the Elite Four (an egg from Erika after Badge 4). The stated rationale: "to ensure it's not too jarring for players who dislike custom Pokémon."

**Community pushback**: Players on PokéCommunity loved the designs ("HOLY CRAP, THAT LOOKED AWESOME") but explicitly requested more access before the E4. The postgame gate frustrated fans who wanted to use custom forms during the main campaign.

**Key difference from LoH**: Radical Red has no narrative justification for its custom forms — they exist purely as gameplay variety. LoH's migration narrative gives in-context *reasons* for regional forms appearing where they do. This makes mid-game placement feel intentional rather than jarring.

## Lazarus's Front-Loading Approach (counterpoint)

Lazarus (v2.0, Feb 2026) includes regional forms in early encounters, with 27+ Pokemon available by the first gym. Players call the mid-game encounter density a strength. Lazarus proves that front-loading forms works when the setting supports it.

## Design Tension

Two valid philosophies exist:
1. **Gate to postgame** (Radical Red): Safer, but players wish they could use forms earlier
2. **Weave into journey** (Lazarus, LoH): Riskier, but more rewarding when narrative supports it

LoH is well-positioned for #2 because the migration story naturally explains why foreign Pokemon appear in Hoenn.

## Implication for C231

When selecting Badge 3-5 species, each should have a clear *environmental adaptation story* tied to its route. This is what separates LoH's approach from "random forms scattered around" — every placement should feel like ecological discovery, not just gameplay variety.
