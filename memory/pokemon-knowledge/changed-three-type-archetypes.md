# Changed Three Type Archetypes — Competitive Precedents

**Cycle**: 288 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Water/Fighting (Mudkip_Hoenn → Swampert_Hoenn)

**Official precedent**: Quaquaval (Gen 9) — 85/120/80/85/75/85 BST 530. OU viable at ~7% usage. Key to its identity: Hidden Ability **Moxie** rewards aggressive play (Atk boost on KO). Signature move Aqua Step boosts Speed on use. Community reception strongly positive — the "snowball" identity (each KO makes it faster and stronger) is what players remember. Torrent alone would have made it forgettable.

**Design lesson for Swampert_Hoenn**: Water/Fighting wants an aggressive identity. If the form keeps Swampert's 110 Atk and loses the Ground-type bulk, it needs an ability that rewards offense, not one that passively tanks. Moxie, Guts, or a custom "contact" ability would fit. Avoid defensive abilities that fight the typing's natural role.

## Grass/Steel (Treecko_Hoenn → Sceptile_Hoenn)

**Competitive archetypes**: Two distinct patterns exist:
1. **Ferrothorn** (defensive wall): 74/94/131/54/116/20. Iron Barbs punishes contact. 9 resistances + Poison immunity. Works because it's slow and bulky. 4x Fire weakness is offset by forced switches.
2. **Kartana** (offensive glass cannon): 59/181/131/59/31/109. Beast Boost snowballs. Works because it's so fast and powerful it kills before Fire moves land.

Sceptile_Hoenn (fast, offensive) maps to the **Kartana archetype**, not Ferrothorn. This means:
- The ability must compensate for the 4x Fire weakness offensively (speed, power, or priority), not defensively
- "Tempered Blade" (crit-boost ability or Steel-type priority) would fit better than defensive options
- Learnset needs Speed control or priority to justify the 4x weakness

## Platinum Redux Lesson (Community Feedback)

Regional forms in Platinum Redux were praised when stats, abilities, AND learnsets all changed cohesively. Changing just typing without coherent ability/learnset adjustment is the most common community criticism of regional forms. LoH's three-tier system (custom abilities + reassignment + signature moves) directly addresses this — the strategy is validated.
