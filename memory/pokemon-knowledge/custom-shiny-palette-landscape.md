# Custom Shiny Palette Landscape

**Cycle**: 281 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: Full Shiny Redesign = Hack Identity, Not Feature

The hacks that successfully redesign shinies treat it as a **core identity**, not a bolt-on feature:

- **SparklingRed** (FireRed): 350+ custom shinies, but the ENTIRE hack revolves around this — shiny odds raised to 1/8, every route designed around shiny hunting. The custom shinies ARE the game. It took enormous scope and the hack's content pacing was built around it.
- **Individual Color Variation** (Platinum): Subtler approach — fixes "obviously bad" shinies (near-identical to base form, ugly colors) while leaving acceptable ones alone. Much smaller scope, well-received.
- **Unbound**: Has custom shinies created by its dev team, but as polish — not the central feature.
- **Alternate Shiny Pokémon** (PokéCommunity resource): Community-shared alternate shiny palettes as a graphics resource pack. Available for ROM hackers to use.

## Scope Reality

Redesigning ALL shiny palettes for ~400 species = ~400 palette files. Even at 5 minutes per palette (design, test, iterate), that's 33+ hours of pure palette work. For a project where shinies aren't the central hook, the ROI is very low — most players will see 0-3 shinies in a playthrough at standard 1/8192 odds.

## What Actually Works

The "fix the worst offenders" approach (Individual Color Variation model) is the community-validated pattern: identify the 20-30 species with genuinely bad shinies (barely visible difference, garish colors), fix those. Maximum impact, minimal scope.
