# Consistency Pass — World Integration Patterns

**Cycle**: 264 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## The "Bloat" Problem (PokeCommunity 2025-2026)

A prominent PokeCommunity thread ("Many rom hacks feel kinda bloated") identifies the core failure mode for hacks with regional forms: forms exist in encounter tables but the world doesn't acknowledge them. Players feel "swamped or cramped" when abundance doesn't serve world-building.

The community consensus: "a thoughtful selection that asks players to engage with the journey through how the world exists, rather than just engaging with gameplay, feels better even if some favorite Pokémon don't make the cut."

**Key pattern**: The hacks praised for regional form integration (Unbound, Imperium, Royal Sapphire) all have NPC dialogue that references the forms by name, describes ecological change, and gives trainers who carry them specific personality around their discovery. Royal Sapphire rewrites "every NPC dialogue to enrich towns and lore" with "every trainer team, move, and held item handcrafted."

## What Players Notice in Consistency

From C253 ship evaluation research (still valid): the #1 complaint is "broken scripts, softlocks, and inconsistencies" — not missing features. Players catch:
- Forms that exist in grass but no NPC ever mentions
- Abilities that don't match the species' combat role (C263 addressed this)
- Trainers who carry a form but say nothing about it

## Unbound's Polish Standard (2025-2026)

Pokémon Unbound remains the community benchmark. Called "the absolute pinnacle of ROM hack development" with "every feature feeling polished and complete." Its polish comes from density of acknowledgment — NPCs, trainers, environmental details all reference the same design decisions.
