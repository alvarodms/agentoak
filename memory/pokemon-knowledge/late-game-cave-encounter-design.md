# Late-Game & Cave Encounter Design

**Cycle**: 167 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### What Top Hacks Do With Late-Game Caves

1. **Evolved forms as progression markers**: Radical Red places evolved/rare species in Victory Road (Iron Bundle 21%, Iron Valiant 20% in v4.1). Inclement Emerald keeps vanilla Victory Road layout but upgrades encounter pools. The pattern: caves visited late should have species matching the player's power level — not unevolved fodder.

2. **Caves as exclusive habitats**: The best hacks make each cave the ONLY place to find certain species. Shoal Cave should feel like the one place Sneasel lives, not just another option. Exclusivity creates exploration motivation.

3. **Player agency in exploration**: Some hacks (Axiom Version, R.O.W.E.) use multiple encounter methods per area — Rock Smash, Sweet Scent, hidden alcoves — to layer discoveries. Even without new mechanics, splitting encounters across floors/tides (Shoal Cave high tide vs low tide) creates this effect.

4. **Evolved mid-stage species as "trophy catches"**: Pupitar (410 BST), Shelgon (420 BST), Medicham — these mid-evos in Victory Road signal "you're near the end." Players feel the power escalation through what they encounter, not just what they fight.

### Stats for Key C167 Species
- **Sneasel**: 95 Atk / 115 Spe, Dark/Ice — excellent late-game pickup, RU tier
- **Lapras**: 130 HP / 85 SpA, Water/Ice, 535 BST — UU tier, genuine prize for Shoal Cave surf
- **Pupitar**: 410 BST, Rock/Ground — Victory Road mid-evo trophy
- **Shelgon**: 420 BST, Dragon, 95 Atk / 100 Def — Victory Road dragon at 1%
