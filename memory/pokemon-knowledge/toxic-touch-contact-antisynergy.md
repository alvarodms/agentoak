# Toxic Touch + Special Attacker — Contact Anti-Synergy

**Cycle**: 240 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Critical Finding: Toxic Touch Rarely Procs on Special Attackers

Toxic Touch (Gen 5 Poison Touch) triggers on **contact moves** — which are almost exclusively physical. Deoxys_Hoenn has 150 SpA / 80 Atk and wants to run special moves. But its planned coverage (Sludge Bomb, Psychic, Shadow Ball, Ice Beam, Thunderbolt) are **all non-contact**.

**Result**: Toxic Touch would be a dead ability on Deoxys_Hoenn's optimal moveset.

## Canonical Poison Touch Users — All Physical/Mixed

Every official Pokémon with Poison Touch is physical or mixed:
- **Grimer/Muk**: ~105 Atk, uses Gunk Shot, Poison Jab (contact)
- **Seismitoad**: 95 Atk mixed, uses Drain Punch, Earthquake (some contact)
- **Toxicroak**: 106 Atk, uses Cross Chop, Poison Jab (contact)

No pure special attacker has ever had Poison Touch in official games. This is unprecedented.

## Options to Resolve

1. **Add physical contact moves to learnset** — Poison Jab, Shadow Claw, etc. 80 Atk is weak but Toxic Touch proc is the point
2. **Redesign Toxic Touch to trigger on any damaging move** — more powerful, no precedent, but solves the problem cleanly
3. **Accept anti-synergy** — Toxic Touch is flavor/switchin deterrent, Pressure is the "real" ability for competitive play
4. **Swap stat spread** — Make Deoxys_Hoenn a physical attacker (150 Atk / 80 SpA) to synergize with contact + Toxic Touch
5. **Use "special contact" concept** — In Gen 4+, a few special moves make contact (e.g., Draining Kiss, Grass Knot). Verify which exist in this hack.

## Recommendation

Option 3 (accept it as flavor) is the path of least resistance but feels like a wasted signature ability. Option 2 (any damaging move triggers it) is the cleanest design — it differentiates from Poison Point AND synergizes with the glass cannon identity. Would make Toxic Touch unique to this hack rather than a Poison Touch clone.
