# Encounter Placement & Postgame Gift Patterns

**Cycle**: 279 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Location-Typed Placement Precedents

**Moon Emerald** systematically places later-gen species at thematically matched locations: Goomy at Meteor Falls (Dragon-themed cave), Vanillish at Shoal Cave (ice environment), Zorua at Mt. Pyre (ghost/dark atmosphere). This validates LoH's approach of Treecko_Hoenn (Steel) at Meteor Falls (mineral deposits), Torchic_Hoenn (Fairy) at Mt. Pyre (spiritual), Mudkip_Hoenn (Fighting) at Shoal Cave (tidal discipline).

**Emerald Double Edition** uses the same locations for legendary encounters: Deoxys in Meteor Falls (Bagon room), Articuno in Shoal Cave. The Bagon room specifically has precedent as a "special encounter" zone in the hacking community.

## Postgame Starter Gift Patterns

- **Emerald: Essence** gives all 3 Johto starters on Dex completion (instead of vanilla's choose-one). More generous but less meaningful.
- **Emerald Squared** gifts a second starter from Birch post-E4.
- **Standard vanilla**: Birch offers choice of Chikorita/Cyndaquil/Totodile after completing Hoenn Dex.

LoH plan replaces Johto starters with the Changed Three in the same gift event. This is clean because the script infrastructure already exists — just swap species constants.

## Discovery Design: Breadcrumbs vs. Blind Hunting

From prior research (C255/C257): 4% encounter rate = ~25 encounters to find. Key patterns:
- **Reborn/Unbound**: NPC remarks on phenomenon casually (environmental storytelling), never as quest directive
- **Lazarus**: DexNav-style chain hunting rewards rarity with engagement loop
- **Gaia**: 10% = "findable naturally," 4% = "hunt for it." Both valid for different design intentions.

LoH has existing Echo NPCs in these areas. Using them to drop casual migration observations ("strange mineral growths in the deeper chambers") creates breadcrumbs without quest markers.
