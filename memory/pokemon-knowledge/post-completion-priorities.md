# Post-Completion Priorities: What Top Hacks Do After "Everything Is Done"

**Cycle**: 74 | **Date**: March 2026

---

## Key Findings

### 2025-2026 Community Consensus on What Makes Hacks "Must-Play"
From reviews of top-rated hacks (Unbound, Radical Red, Lazarus, Emerald Legacy):

1. **Stability > features** — players abandon hacks with crashes/softlocks. Polish wins over feature count.
2. **QoL is table stakes** — reusable TMs, auto-run, physical/special split indicators are expected, not bonus.
3. **Move category icons specifically** — every competitive-oriented hack (Radical Red, Inclement Emerald, Emerald Imperium) shows physical/special indicators. Players treat this as essential for P/S split hacks.
4. **Postgame structure matters** — Unbound's 80+ missions, tiered rematches, and battle facilities are the gold standard. Players want clear postgame goals, not just "catch legendaries."
5. **Consistency across the entire game** — no dead zones. If routes are polished but early areas aren't, the contrast is jarring.

### What Players DON'T Want
- Half-baked features that feel incomplete
- Feature creep that introduces bugs
- Overworld following Pokémon that's buggy (better to skip than do poorly)

## Application to LoH
With trainers complete everywhere, the highest-value next moves are:
