# Mid-Game Trainer Progression Design

**Cycle**: 147 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Findings

### What Top Hacks Do With Route Trainers (Badges 3-6)

1. **Graduated complexity, not just levels** — Radical Red and Inclement Emerald don't just raise levels on mid-game trainers; they introduce held items, coverage moves, and 2-mon synergy (e.g., a Hiker with Sandstorm Hippopotas + Sand Rush Sandslash). Early-game trainers teach type matchups; mid-game trainers teach team synergy.

2. **Route trainers as gym previews** — Best hacks use route trainers to *prepare* players for the upcoming gym. Routes before Flannery should feature Fire-type trainers or Pokemon with fire coverage so the player knows what's coming. This is pedagogical design.

3. **Double battles as mid-game staple** — Radical Red, Inclement Emerald, and Emerald Imperium all increase double battle frequency in mid-game routes. Singles are for early game; doubles introduce positional strategy when players have 4-5 team members.

4. **Held item escalation curve** — Community consensus: Oran/Sitrus berries on early trainers, strategic items (Eviolite, Choice Band, Leftovers) starting around Badge 3-4. Gen 3 limits apply (no Focus Sash, Life Orb).

5. **Avoid "wall of Geodudes" syndrome** — The most common mid-game criticism is repetitive trainer teams. Each route should have a distinct identity — terrain-appropriate species, not just level-scaled copies of the same teams.
