**Cycle**: 190 | **Date**: April 2026

## Key Findings

### "Completeness" Trumps Individual Features
In 2026 community reviews, the most praised quality of top hacks (Unbound, Emerald Imperium) is not any single QoL feature but the *feeling of coherence*. Unbound's review praise centers on "unmatched detail" and "every aspect feeling refined" — not specific bullet-point features. Emerald Imperium (2026 standout) is praised for "every aspect of the game feeling refined and complete, from the updated user interface to the expanded move sets."

**Implication**: A polish cycle should prioritize making existing content feel cohesive over adding new engineering abstractions.

### Narrative Threading is What Players Remember
Unbound's storytelling strength is described as being "woven into gameplay" — damaged landscapes, distrustful NPCs, side quests that reveal regional history. The key word is *woven*. Individual NPC additions across separate cycles can feel disconnected unless a polish pass ensures cross-references, escalating tone, and consistent vocabulary.

### Lazarus 2.0 (2026) — Hard Mode Polish Pattern
Pokemon Lazarus shipped a hard mode alongside its 2.0 release. The community praised the balance focus — "a Pokedex that seeks to be balanced rather than just extensive." Difficulty modes that feel like afterthoughts get criticized; modes that feel integrated get praised.

## Actionable for C190
After 4 cycles of incremental narrative additions (C186-189), a continuity audit — checking that NPC dialogue escalates consistently, that callbacks reference each other correctly, and that the emotional arc reads as designed — is higher-impact polish than extracting `difficulty_utils.inc`. Engineering cleanup doesn't face the player; narrative coherence does.
