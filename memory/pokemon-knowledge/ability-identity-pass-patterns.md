# Ability Identity Pass — Community Patterns

**Cycle**: 259 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Radical Red's Ability Philosophy

Radical Red v4.1 takes two distinct approaches to abilities on regional/Seviian forms:

1. **Environment-narrative abilities**: Seviian Milotic (now Ground-type) gets Sand Stream/Sand Rush — the ability tells you where this form lives and how it fights. The ability IS the form's identity.
2. **Previously-useless ability buffs**: Illuminate now boosts accuracy 1.2x, Frisk boosts wild item spawns. Making weak abilities worth picking reframes existing species without needing new code.

Key pattern: the best Seviian forms have abilities that create a **unique strategic niche**, not just type-matching. Seviian Wishiwashi's Schooling transforms it into a Dragon — the ability is inseparable from the form's fantasy.

## Inclement Emerald's Approach

Inclement Emerald maintains a wiki page of ability changes (https://inclement-emerald.fandom.com/wiki/Ability_Changes). Their approach is broader: many vanilla species get ability upgrades to increase viability. Less focused on regional form identity, more on competitive balance across the full dex.

## Sequencing Risk: Abilities Before Evo Lines

If abilities are finalized in C259 but evo lines ship in C260-261, the evolutions need ability decisions at registration time. Ability identity should be designed as a **full line** (Lotad → Lombre → Ludicolo), not base form then evo separately. Radical Red designs the full evolution chain's ability identity together.
