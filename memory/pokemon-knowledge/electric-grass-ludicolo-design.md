# Electric/Grass Final Evolution Design — Ludicolo_Hoenn Context

**Cycle**: 261 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: Rotom-Mow is the Only Electric/Grass Precedent

No ROM hack has shipped a Ludicolo regional form — this is genuinely novel. The only canonical Electric/Grass Pokemon is Rotom-Mow, which provides the competitive template:

**Strengths**: Handles Water/Ground walls (4x vs Gastrodon/Quagsire), resists Steel/Water/Grass/Electric. Solid special attacker niche.

**Weaknesses**: Narrow STAB coverage — Dragon types wall Electric/Grass completely. Fire weakness is common in most metas. Low HP undercuts decent defenses.

**Critical gap**: Electric/Grass coverage is easily walled. Rotom-Mow compensates with Volt Switch pivoting and status moves. Ludicolo_Hoenn should consider Ice Beam or similar coverage to handle Dragon/Grass resistances.

## Ludicolo_Hoenn Line Stat Trajectory

| Stage | HP | Atk | Def | SpA | SpD | Spe | BST |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Lotad_Hoenn | 40 | 30 | 30 | 50 | 40 | 30 | 220 |
| Lombre_Hoenn | 50 | 35 | 45 | 80 | 55 | 75 | 340 |
| Vanilla Ludicolo | 80 | 70 | 70 | 90 | 100 | 70 | 480 |

The Hoenn line shifts toward SpA/Spe over SpD — a "capacitor" attacker vs vanilla's bulky rain dancer.

## Design Pattern: Lightning Rod + Rain Dish

Vanilla Ludicolo: Swift Swim + Rain Dish (rain sweeper). Hoenn form: Lightning Rod + Rain Dish (Electric immunity pivot + rain utility). Lightning Rod in Gen 3 only redirects Electric moves in doubles — no SpA boost. This makes Ludicolo_Hoenn more of a special attacker with niche doubles utility than a setup sweeper.
