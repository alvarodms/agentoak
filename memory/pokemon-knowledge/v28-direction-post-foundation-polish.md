# v2.8 Direction — Post-Foundation Polish & Playability Patterns

**Cycle**: 295 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Key Finding: The "Playable Polish Gap"

After a long content/feature streak (v2.5-v2.7 was 20+ cycles of species registration, abilities, and signature moves), the top hacks that succeed are the ones that shift to playability polish before adding more content. The pattern from Unbound, Emerald Imperium, and Inclement Emerald:

1. **Build the content foundation** (custom forms, abilities, moves) — DONE for Legends of Hoenn
2. **Polish the player experience** (encounter rates, repel QoL, animation quality, difficulty tuning) — THIS IS THE GAP
3. **Then expand further** (more forms, quests, narrative)

## QoL Features Players Expect in 2026

The decomp scene has converged on a standard QoL package:
- **Repel continuation prompt** (BW-style): Re:Emerald and Emerald Plus both ship this. Community considers it baseline.
- **Encounter rate reduction**: Re:Emerald ensures no species below 5% encounter rate. Multiple player reports call vanilla 11% land rate frustrating.
- **Cleanse Tag as infinite repel**: Emerald++ does this — controversial but popular.

## Visual Polish Matters More Than Expected

Emerald Imperium (2026) is praised specifically for visual refinement — updated sprites, animations, UI. Players explicitly cite "feeling polished" as a reason to choose it over mechanically similar hacks. The battle animation investment (currently deferred 3 cycles in tech-debt-backlog) aligns with this trend.
