# Meteor Falls Event Design — Bagon Colony

**Cycle**: 153 | **Date**: April 2026

---

## Bagon Stats & Movepool (Gen 3)

- **Type**: Dragon | **BST**: 300 | **Ability**: Rock Head
- **Base stats**: 45/75/60/40/30/50
- **Key level-up moves**: Headbutt (17), Ember (25), Dragon Breath (33), Crunch (41), Dragon Claw (49)
- **Notable egg moves**: Dragon Dance, Hydro Pump, Thrash
- **TMs available**: Dragon Claw, Brick Break, Flamethrower, Fire Blast, Aerial Ace, Rock Tomb
- **Rock Head synergy**: Double-Edge (tutor/level 53) has no recoil — a strong design hook for the special encounter

## Design Considerations for the Event

**Level 25-28 Bagon moveset options** (per the roadmap):
- At level 25, Bagon naturally knows: Rage, Bite, Leer, Headbutt, Focus Energy, Ember
- Dragon Breath (lv 33) would need to be added as a bonus move — justified as "migration Bagon are unusually strong"
- Consider giving the special encounter Bagon a rare held item (Dragon Fang boosts Dragon moves 10%)

**What works in ROM hack dungeon events** (from research):
1. **Unbound model**: Missions/side content reward exploration with unique encounters. Multiple OW sprites in a cluster create a "discovery" moment.
2. **CosmicEmerald**: Lapras at Meteor Falls waterfall on Mondays — day-gated special encounters in caves are a proven pattern.
3. **Player agency**: The roadmap's "Bagon scatter, one remains" design gives the player a choice moment — approach or don't. This is better than a forced cutscene.
4. **Scaling payoff**: Bagon -> Shelgon -> Salamence is one of Gen 3's most aspirational evolution lines (pseudo-legendary). Getting one at level 25-28 mid-game is a meaningful gameplay reward, not just a narrative beat.

## Risk: Encounter Balance

A level 25-28 Bagon with Dragon Breath is quite strong for post-Badge 4. Salamence (BST 600) won't come until level 50, but Shelgon (BST 420) at level 30 is solid. This is comparable to getting a Larvitar early in many ROM hacks — aspirational but not game-breaking given the slow evolution.
