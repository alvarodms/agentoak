## Pillar 2 — Mid-Game Journey Polish

### Target Level Curve

Badges 1-3: Lv12→19. Badge 4 (Flannery 29): 26-29. Badge 5 (Norman 31): 28-31. Badge 6 (Winona 33): 30-33. Badges 7-8: 37-46. E4: 48-55 vs leaders at 56-58.

### Encounter Audit Targets

- **Routes 110-113**: Verify 8+ species per route, 2+ migrants, good level spread
- **Routes 119-120**: Long routes — high variety needed. Already have Togetic corridor.
- Design principle: No route should be "Zigzagoon + Poochyena + one interesting thing"

### Narrative Beats (3 NPCs — migration intensifying)

**Beat 1 — Lavaridge Town** (pre-Flannery): Traveling researcher notes first-wave babies are being followed by evolved forms. Something is pushing them harder. Foreshadows Second Wave.

**Beat 2 — Fortree City** (pre-Winona): Bird keeper notes foreign species displacing native Swellow/Taillow in the canopy. Winona adapting her gym strategy.

**Beat 3 — Weather Institute** (post-Aqua battle): Scientist detects energy signature from Cave of Origin growing stronger. Tells player to inform Birch. Foreshadows postgame quest.

### Double Battles (2 new trainers)

- **Route 110**: Twins with Growlithe Lv16 + Poliwag Lv16 (migration Fire/Water combo)
- **Route 119**: Breeder pair with Houndour Lv27 + Snubbull Lv27 (Dark/Fairy migration)

## Pillar 4 — Migration Tracker Quest

### Concept
After completing Birch Research Quest, Birch asks the Champion to catalogue migration species for a field guide.

### Structure (3 stages, flag-gated)

**Stage 1 — First Wave Pioneers**: Catch any 5 of: Growlithe/Vulpix, Meowth, Poliwag, Nidoran♂, Teddiursa, Phanpy, Mareep, Machop, Houndour, Ralts
→ Reward: Rare Candy ×3

**Stage 2 — Second Wave Apex**: Catch any 3 of: Tyranitar, Dragonite, Salamence, Garchomp, Lucario, Weavile
→ Reward: PP Max ×2

**Stage 3 — Regional Specialists**: Catch 1 water migrant (Corsola/Lanturn), 1 cave migrant (Riolu/Gible), 1 forest migrant (Scyther/Heracross)
→ Reward: Shell Bell (if not obtained) or Master Ball

### Implementation
- Flags: `FLAG_MIGRATION_TRACKER_*` (0x028-0x02B)
- Script: Birch Lab, branching on caught Pokédex entries
- Uses existing Birch Quest infrastructure — no new maps needed
