# v3.11 "Still warm" (spec C393, built C394)

During the Mt. Pyre summit orb theft, while the screen is black, a lit lantern appears beside the west headstone at 21,16, a tile the player walked past on the way up. One box, no name, no reward, no new persistent flag. Hour ~11 (Badge 6→7), on the critical path (the Magma Emblem is given in the same scene). Hypothesis (C393): "If the player finds signs of someone who was there before them and is gone now, with no fight and no explanation, Hours 10-15 shift from triumph to unease. If the player keeps walking without stopping to read it, the beat failed." The headless suite proves presence/timing only; "stops to read it" waits for a human report.

**Never extinguished, never explained**: the lantern stays lit through the postgame. No NPC, Isolde, Keeper or Field Notes line ever mentions it until a retrospective explicitly reopens it (creative 393).

## Fog readability check (C394, screenshots viewed)
- **PASS, variant 1 shipped** (the redraw fallback was not needed). Both `mt-pyre-lantern-fog.png` (boot at 22,16) and `mt-pyre-lantern-walkdown.png` (after the real theft, at 23,12: the lantern is at the bottom edge of the screen beside the headstone) were read, the fog one also at 6x zoom: dark navy outline, top ring, roof, bright yellow core, amber sill, beside the headstone at 20,16; as crisply defined as the player sprite, not an item ball or smudge.
- Fog note: the spec's "sprites blended ~75% toward RGB(28,31,28)" (field_weather.c:674-676 is only for `lightenedFogSpritePals`; :842 BlendPalette 12/16 is the weather-change state) did NOT show in the steady-state screenshots: the lantern's iron reads navy and the core yellow, only lightly washed. Value contrast is still the safe design rule; never fix fog readability by changing the palette slot.
