# Legends of Hoenn — v1.0 Release

## What Is This?

Hoenn has changed. A mass migration of rare and powerful Pokémon from across the world has swept into the region, upending its ecosystem and drawing out forces — both human and legendary — that had long been dormant. You arrive in Littleroot at the perfect moment: just in time to choose one of three migratory species as your partner and step into a Hoenn that doesn't go easy on you.

**Legends of Hoenn** is a Pokémon Emerald ROM hack built on one promise: every Pokémon you encounter is worth catching. Every trainer is a real fight. The world has changed, and you get to explore it.

---

## Key Features

### Choose Your Legend
Begin with one of three pseudo-legendary starter lines — each with a distinct identity:
- **Larvitar** (→ Pupitar → Tyranitar) — The Crusher. Slow to evolve, unstoppable when it does.
- **Bagon** (→ Shelgon → Salamence) — The Dreamer. Classic Dragon power on a deadline.
- **Dratini** (→ Dragonair → Dragonite) — The Legend. Graceful, iconic, worth the wait.

Your rival takes the starter with a type advantage, with a full team built around it.

### A Transformed Hoenn
Every wild encounter table in the game has been redesigned — all 73 routes, all 34 dungeons, the Safari Zone. Houndour packs prowl early routes. Larvitar haunt the mountain paths. Dratini drift through the rivers. Lapras cross the southern seas. This isn't a random loot table: it's an ecosystem.

### Trainers Worth Fighting
All 8 gym leaders carry thematic powerhouse teams with strategic held items, tuned to the migration's new power curve. The Elite Four, rival, villain bosses, and Champion Wallace have all been rebuilt around the same philosophy. No pushover teams. Every badge is earned.

### The Migration Speaks
The story knows what happened to Hoenn. Professor Birch opens by studying the phenomenon. Every gym leader references the ecological shift in their pre-battle dialogue. Maxie and Archie's motivations are reframed around the legendary species the migration has drawn out. Your rival acknowledges the changed world with every encounter. The world is alive.

### Quality of Life
- **Reusable TMs** — TMs are not consumed on use. Build freely.
- **Auto-run** — Running is the default. Hold B to walk.
- **Wild Pokémon hold items** — 164 species carry thematic items in the wild. Farming has purpose.
